# Who gets into your space

A plain-language companion to [Is my data safe?](is-my-data-safe.md). Your shared space starts as just yours.
This page is about the one decision that changes that — inviting someone — and what happens on its own
afterwards.

## Inviting someone

You invite by **email address** — if you are an owner of the space; inviting, and everything under
*Invitations you have already sent* below, is for owners only. That is your one decision: who is allowed in.

The invitation is then **mailed to that person**. It is addressed to them, so it is not yours to pass on — there
is no link on your screen to copy, forward or paste into a chat. That is deliberate. An invitation is an
admission ticket to your space, and a ticket that travels through whoever happens to be looking at your screen
is a ticket that can end up with the wrong person.

They set up their own account from that mail, and land in their **private space** at first.

## Then the key is handed over — by a person, not by us

Being let in and being able to *read* anything are two different steps in veyq, because the server never holds
the key to your space.

So once the new person has joined, the key is handed over **automatically**: any member's device — yours or
someone else's — seals a copy of the key to them as soon as it's online. From that moment they see everything in
the space. The sealing happens **on a member's device**, straight to the newcomer; the server only passes along
sealed data it cannot read.

That is why a new member waits briefly in their private space until *any* of you is next online. In an active
space this is usually moments. It is the price of the server never being able to let someone in behind your
back — and we think that is the right trade.

## Invitations you have already sent

You can see who you have invited but who has not joined yet, have their invitation **emailed again** if it never
arrived, and cancel an invitation you sent by mistake. None of that touches anyone who is already in your space.

A re-sent invitation goes to them, not to you — same as the first one, for the same reason.

## If your space can't send email

Because the invitation only travels by email, a space with no email set up cannot invite anyone. Rather than
minting an invitation nobody will ever receive, veyq refuses and says so. Email is set up **per space, by an
owner**, in the space's settings under **Email** — not by whoever runs the server. If you are an owner, that is
where to go; the same setting is what lets your members reset a forgotten password.
