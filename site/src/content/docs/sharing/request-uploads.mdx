Sometimes you want photos from people without showing them anything, for example from all the guests of a wedding.
An upload link lets them add, and nothing more: they see neither the Memory nor what others added.

1. Open the Memory (or a chapter) and choose **Share** at the top of its page.
2. Choose **New share**, and under **What can people do?** choose **Add photos only**. A name, a note, a password and an
   expiry date are optional, as for any [link](/veyq/sharing/create-a-link/).
3. Choose **Create link**, then **Copy link**, and send it.

<Screenshot id="sharing-request-upload" shows="The New share form with Add photos only chosen under What can people do? and its explanation" />

Whoever opens the link gives a name, chooses **Add photos** and sees their own uploads only while the page is open. You
see everything they added in the Memory, under **Contributions**. Upload links are listed in the same **Share** window as
your other links, marked **Add photos only**.

For files rather than photos, choose **Add files only** in a folder's **Share** instead: it creates a
[drop box](/veyq/depot/drop-boxes/), which works the same way, with a folder of its own for each person.

Guests' photos are locked in their own browser before they are sent, with a key that comes from the link:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/#when-you-share)
