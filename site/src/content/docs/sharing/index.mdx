You can hand something to anyone, a grandparent, a friend, a group chat, as a **link**. They open it in a browser: no
account, nothing to install. The link shows only what you shared, never the rest of your space, and you can switch it
off at any time.

## What you can share

- **A folder**, with everything in it: files, Memories, further folders.
- **A single file.** The folder it sits in stays private.
- **A Memory**, or one of its chapters.

## Three kinds of links

- **View only**: they can look and download, and change nothing.
- **View + contribute** (on a Memory: **View + add photos**): they can look and add their own things. Whether you see
  this choice depends on your server.
- **Add only**: they can hand things in, but see nothing. On a Memory this is **Request upload**; for files it is a
  [drop box](/veyq/depot/drop-boxes/).

## What you can do

- [Create a link](/veyq/sharing/create-a-link/), with a note, a password or an expiry date
- [Ask people to add photos](/veyq/sharing/request-uploads/) without letting them see anything
- [Change, switch off, renew and delete links](/veyq/sharing/manage-links/)
- [See what a guest sees](/veyq/sharing/what-guests-see/)

The key that opens a link sits in the part of the address after the `#`, which a browser never sends to a server. So
people with the link can open what you shared, and the person running the server still cannot. A link cannot be
unseen, though: switching it off stops future access, but not what someone already saved:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/#when-you-share)
