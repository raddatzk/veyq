Open what you shared and choose **Share** again. The window lists every link, upload links included, with its role, when it was created and, if set, when it expires; badges say **Note**, **Password** or **By another
member**. Links that are switched off are folded away underneath. A folder's window does not list the drop boxes
created in it: their links are on each [drop box](/veyq/depot/drop-boxes/).

<Screenshot id="sharing-link-list" shows="The share window with one link and its Copy link, Edit, Renew link and Revoke buttons, and the folded section of switched-off links" />

- **Copy link** copies it again.
- **Edit** changes the name, the note, the password or the expiry, and on a folder or file also the role. The address
  stays the same.
  If the link has a password, it is filled in there; the eye next to it shows it, so you can look it up again later.
- **Revoke** switches the link off at once. What guests added stays with you.
- **Re-activate** switches a revoked link back on, with everything added while it was off.
- **Renew link** makes a new address and deletes the old one; hand out the new link. A link keeps showing what is
  added, also after a space got a new lock, so renewing it or switching it off is what closes it to someone who left
  the space or secured their account. Such a link shows **Needs renewing**. On a Memory, guests keep what they added
  but give their name again.
- **Delete link** removes a switched-off link for good. You can delete your own links, and anyone's once its creator
  has left the space.

Switching a link off, renewing it, or its expiry passing gives what is below the link a new lock, so the old link
opens nothing that comes later either. This happens by itself in the background; until it is done, adding
something there may take a moment longer. An upload link needs no new lock: it can only add, never open
anything. Putting what you shared in the trash only pauses its links: nothing gets a new lock, and once you restore it,
the links show it again.

You can change only the links you created; another member's link shows **By another member**.
When a member is removed from the space or from a group, the links they made there switch off at that moment. Any
remaining member can then renew such a link, which makes the new one theirs, or delete it.

## Switch off every link at once

If a link may have reached the wrong hands and you do not know which, the owner of the space can switch off all of
them: **Settings → Members**, under **Links**, choose **Turn off every link** and confirm. This switches off every
link in the space, for folders, files, Memories and drop boxes. Each one comes back only by renewing it (a drop box
gets a new link instead), so nobody can quietly switch the old address on again. The same button is in a group's
settings for the group's links.

Switching off and deleting take effect on the server; the link's key never reached it in the first place:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/#when-you-share)
