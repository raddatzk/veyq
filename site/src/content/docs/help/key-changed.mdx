title: "A key changed"

When a member shows **Key changed**, the server has started handing out a different key for them than the one your app
trusted. Most of the time that person simply [replaced their keys](/veyq/account/replace-your-keys/). It can also be
someone trying to pass off a key as theirs, which is exactly why veyq stops and asks.

1. Ask the person directly, outside veyq (a call, in person), whether they replaced their keys.
2. Choose **Review key** on their profile or in the member list, and compare the **Current** number with the one on
   their own profile.
3. If it is really them, choose **Accept new key**. If not, or you cannot reach them, choose **Refuse**.

Until you accept, your device hands that person no key and does not add them to a group, so nothing new reaches a key
you have not checked. Step by step: [Verify a person](/veyq/account/verify-a-person/#when-a-key-has-changed).

If you replaced your own keys, the others see this about you. Ask them to check with you, and you are back in once one
of them accepts.

Only a key your own device has accepted is trusted; the server cannot change that quietly:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
