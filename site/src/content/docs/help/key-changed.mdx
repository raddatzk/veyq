When a member shows **Can't confirm this key**, the server is handing out a key for them that does not follow from the
one your app knew. When someone [secures their account](/account/replace-your-keys/), their new key is signed by
their previous one and your app accepts it without asking you; so this is not that. It can be someone trying to pass
off a key as theirs. It also shows for a member who is not an owner of the space and came in without an invitation an
owner signed: every invitation leaves such a record, so a member without one is someone only the server could have
added.

1. Ask the person directly, outside veyq (a call, in person), whether something happened to their account.
2. Compare your safety numbers in person: [Verify a person](/account/verify-a-person/#compare-a-safety-number).

Until their key follows from the one your app knew, your device hands that key nothing: no space key, no group. If a
space gets a new lock meanwhile, they show **Waiting for someone who can confirm their key**, and another member whose
app confirms their key passes it on. Nobody's adding or posting waits for it, and there is no button to accept the key
by hand. More: [Verify a person](/account/verify-a-person/#when-a-key-cant-be-confirmed).

If you secured your own account and someone sees this about you, compare your safety numbers with them in person.

Only a key your own device can trace back to the one it knew is trusted; the server cannot change that quietly:
[Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
