If you see something in a space or through a link that is illegal or against the terms, you can tell the people who
run the server. Only its moderators can read your report. Nobody else learns of it — not the person who shared it, not
the owner of the space.

## Report

1. Choose **Report**:
   - on a folder, a Memory or a file: in its menu, or in its header when it is open;
   - on several at once: choose **Select**, tick them, and choose **Report** in the bar. One report holds up to 100;
     for more, the dialog offers to report the folder that holds them instead;
   - on a photo or video: in the lightbox, the flag button;
   - on a link that only lets you upload: **Report this link** at the bottom of its page.
2. Choose **What is it?**: child sexual abuse material, terrorist content, a threat to someone's life or safety, other
   illegal content, or a breach of the terms. For child sexual abuse material you do not need to give your name.
3. Say **Why are you reporting it?** and tick that what you say is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge.
4. If you want a receipt now and the decision later, give **Your email address**. It is optional.
5. Choose **Send report**. The dialog shows a reference and the time. Keep them if you want to refer to your report.

If **Report** is not there, the server has no moderation key yet, and there is nobody to report to.

## What happens then

- **Only the moderators can read it.** Your device seals your text, your address and the keys to exactly what you
  reported, so that only the moderators of this server can open them. The server sees which items were reported, when,
  and in which category — nothing you wrote. Nothing else of the space opens with those keys.
- **Nothing is blocked by a report.** A person decides. Until then the item stays where it was, for everyone who had it.
- **What you reported is kept for the moderators.** If it is deleted or changed after your report, the moderators still
  see it as it was when you reported it — for up to 90 days, while they decide.
- **The moderators are told** that there is a new case and its category, never what it is or where. Urgent categories
  reach them at once.
- **Your address** goes to the server once, for the receipt it mails you; it keeps only the copy the moderators alone
  can read.
