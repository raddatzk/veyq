## "This space is temporarily offline"

The person who runs your server has taken your space offline for the moment, for example while looking into a report.
Its content is safe and unchanged; it just cannot be opened, by members or through links. Once they bring it back,
everything is where you left it. If you belong to more than one space, the space switcher marks this one
**Temporarily offline**. Ask whoever runs your server when it is back.

A space that is being deleted looks the same. Then you also got an email saying so and naming the date: on that date
everything in the space is erased, unless whoever runs your server stops the deletion before. Your account stays, and so
do your other spaces.

## "This instance can't be reached right now"

veyq cannot talk to its server: your internet connection is down, or the server is. Check your connection and try
again later. Nothing on your device is lost; your keys stay where they were.

## "This instance isn't set up yet"

Nobody has created the first account on this server, so there is nothing to sign in to. Whoever runs the server sets it
up on its own administration surface, not on this page. If you were sent this address, you may have the wrong one.

Taking a space offline does not open it to anyone: the person running the server still cannot read what is in it:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
