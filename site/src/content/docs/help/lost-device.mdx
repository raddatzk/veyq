A lost or stolen device may still be able to open your space: if it was signed in, it holds your key. Take it away.

## From another device you are signed in on

1. Open **Settings → Devices and sessions**.
2. On the lost device's row, choose **Sign out + remove key** and confirm. If the row does not offer it, choose the
   sign-out icon, then remove its key under **Devices with a saved key**.

Whoever holds it then needs your password **and** your Secret Key: [Devices and sessions](/veyq/account/devices-and-sessions/).

## On a borrowed device, without your Emergency Kit

1. On the sign-in page, enter your email and password and choose **I don't have my Secret Key with me**.
2. Choose **Sign out everywhere, including here** and confirm.

Every device is signed out, and any device that was set to stay signed in loses its saved key. Devices that kept a
key you need your password for are cut off from the server but are not stripped of that key; remove them later, from
a device where you are unlocked.

## If someone might already have used it

Signing out stops what comes next but does not take back what the device already holds. If you think someone opened
your space with it, [secure your account](/veyq/account/replace-your-keys/) as well; that needs your password and your
Emergency Kit.

Removing a key takes away what the server was keeping for that device; it never reaches into the device itself:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
