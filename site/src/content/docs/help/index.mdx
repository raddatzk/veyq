Something is not working, or something is gone? Find your situation:

- **I forgot my password.** [Forgot your password](/account/forgot-password/): you need a link by email and your
  Emergency Kit.
- **I lost my Emergency Kit.** [Lost Emergency Kit](/help/lost-emergency-kit/)
- **A device was lost or stolen.** [Lost device](/help/lost-device/)
- **Someone shows "Can't confirm this key".** [A key can't be confirmed](/help/key-changed/)
- **veyq asks me to "Start a list over".** [Start a list over](/help/start-a-list-over/)
- **My space is offline, or veyq can't be reached.** [Offline or unreachable](/help/offline/)
- **I saw something illegal or against the terms.** [Report something](/help/report-something/)
- **My invitation link doesn't work.** It may have expired, or someone used it already; ask whoever invited you for
  a new one: [Accept an invitation](/getting-started/accept-an-invitation/#if-it-does-not-work)
- **A link someone sent me says it isn't available.** It was switched off, has expired or is incomplete; ask for a
  new one: [What a guest sees](/sharing/what-guests-see/)
- **Something these pages describe is missing.** Whoever runs your server can switch some features off, such as links
  for people without an account, groups, or adding a device from another one. veyq then does not show them at all,
  rather than letting them fail when you try.

Nobody can look into your space to help you, not the person who owns it and not the person who runs the server. That
is why recovering always runs through what only you hold: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
