Without your kit, veyq cannot make you a new one: the Secret Key in it is not stored anywhere else, not on your devices
and not on the server, and a new kit comes only from [securing your account](/account/replace-your-keys/), which
asks for that Secret Key. What you can still do depends on whether you are signed in somewhere.

## You are still signed in on a device

Nothing is lost yet. That device keeps opening your space as long as it keeps its key and you know your password.

1. Make sure you have more than one way in: [add another device](/account/adding-another-device/) from the one you
   are signed in on. The new device needs neither your password nor the kit.
2. Look after your password: write it down somewhere safe.
3. Keep **Stay signed in on this device** on for a device you hold, and avoid signing it out: a device that signs out
   asks for the Secret Key the next time.

Without the kit you cannot reset a forgotten password and cannot [secure your account](/account/replace-your-keys/).
If you also forget your password, or the last of your devices loses its key, everything you keep is gone.

## You are not signed in anywhere

If you still know your password, try to sign in on a device you used before: if that device was not set to stay signed
in and still remembers you, your email and password are enough ([Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in/)).

If that does not work either, the data cannot be opened any more. Nobody can bring it back, not the person who owns your
space and not the person who runs the server.

## Someone else may have your kit

If you still have it but someone else may have seen or copied it, [secure your account](/account/replace-your-keys/).
That needs your password and the kit you have now, and it gives you a new kit with a new Secret Key; the old one stops
working.

The same thing that keeps everyone else out also keeps rescue out: there is no master key and no back door:
[Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
