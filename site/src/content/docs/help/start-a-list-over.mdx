Your device keeps two lists for your account: **the keys you trust** and **your device names**. Both are locked and kept
on the server, and your device checks every copy it gets back. If the server returns an older copy than your device
has already seen, or one that cannot be opened, your device refuses it. Then you see a warning, for example that device
names are unavailable, with a link **Start this list over**.

## Wait first

Often the list comes back by itself. Only start over if it does not. The server cannot start a list over for you, but
it could hold one back until you do, so do not let a warning hurry you.

## Start it over

1. Open **Settings → Start a list over**. It is listed only while this device refuses a list.
2. Choose **Start over…** next to the list.
3. Read what it costs, tick **I understand what starting over costs.** and choose **Start over**.

What starting over costs:

- **The keys you trust:** every contact is new to you again. Every safety number you compared is forgotten, and a key
  that changed in the meantime is accepted as new, without the warning that would have told you. Compare the
  important ones again: [Verify a person](/veyq/account/verify-a-person/).
- **Your device names:** every device is unnamed until it next signs in, and removing another device's saved key is
  not offered until then. Every session is still listed and can still be ended.

These lists are why a server cannot quietly roll back what your device has learnt; refusing an old copy is the device
doing its job: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
