The veyq [app](/getting-started/install-the-app/) can keep content on your device, so you can open it without a
connection. It keeps two kinds:

- **What you viewed recently:** pictures, previews, the part of a video you played, the files you opened, and a Pot's
  entries with the receipts you looked at, up to a storage limit you set — on Android, Windows and Linux also the map pictures you looked at, which are never shown
  offline ([Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/#maps-only-when-you-turn-them-on)). When the limit is reached,
  what you have not looked at for the longest goes first.
- **What you make available offline:** a Memory, a Chapter, a folder, a file or a Pot, downloaded whole and kept until
  you turn it off.

Beside them it keeps your spaces' member lists, so the people of a Memory are named offline too, and the list of each
space's photos and videos, so [search](/spaces/search/) finds them offline.

Everything stays locked. The app keeps it exactly as your server stores it and locks it once more for this device, so
whoever copies the files learns nothing from them. The browser keeps nothing.

:::note[Only on a device that stays signed in]
The app keeps content only for an account that stays signed in on this device. Where you need your Secret Key every
time, **Storage on this device** in Settings says: *Content is kept for offline use only on a device that stays signed
in to this account.* How a device stays signed in: [Devices and sessions](/account/devices-and-sessions/).
:::

## Make something available offline

1. Open the Memory, Chapter, folder, file or Pot.
2. Open the menu in its header (**More**) and choose **Available offline**.
3. The app says how much it keeps: **Make available offline?** Choose **Make available**.

While it downloads, the header says how far it is (**Downloading … of …**), then **On this device**. On Android the
app asks the first time whether it may show notifications: the download's progress shows there. If your device runs out of space, it says **Paused: not enough space on this device**.

A photo is kept as its preview, the way the app shows it; its original is not. A video, a Memory's song, a GPX track and a file are kept whole, and
so are the covers and a Memory's banner inside. A Pot is kept with its entries and every receipt. What others add or
change while you are online is downloaded too.

To undo it, choose **Available offline** again. It is turned off at once, without a question.

## Set how much is kept

Open the menu (on a computer, the sidebar), choose **Settings** at its foot, and go to **Storage on this device**.

- **Storage limit:** type a size, choose **MB** or **GB** and then **Set limit**. On Android, choose **Storage limit**
  first; on Linux, set it with the check mark at the field's end. It starts at 2 GB on a phone and 10 GB on a computer.
  A lower limit takes effect at once.
- **Off** keeps nothing you only viewed (on Linux and Windows, it is the last entry in the unit's list). What you made
  available offline still stays.
- **Recently viewed**, **Available offline** and **Free on this device** show the sizes.
- Below them, each item available offline, with what it holds and **Remove from this device**.
- **Clear cache** removes what you only viewed. **Remove all offline content** removes everything, including what you
  made available offline, and asks first.

What you make available offline always stays, even beyond the limit. Then nothing else is kept. A video larger than a
quarter of the limit is not kept just for having been watched. When your device has less than 5 % space left, the app
keeps nothing new.

## When you are offline

The app shows **Offline — content as of** and the time your server last confirmed what it shows. You can look at
everything that is kept and play its videos and music; anything else says **Not available offline**. No map is shown
offline (*The map needs a connection.*); a Memory's map view still lists its tracks. A Memory's people are not shown offline. A Pot
opens with its entries and balances as of the time named; a receipt you never looked at says **Not available offline**.

You can also start the app without a connection: unlock it as you always do. Once the server can be reached again,
the app notices it by itself within about a minute.

## Changes made offline

On a device that stays signed in, you can go on adding and changing offline: add photos and videos to a Memory, make a
Memory, a Chapter or a folder, add a file, add, edit or delete a Pot's entries with their receipts, correct a photo's
capture time, mark, rename and move to the trash. Each change shows where you made it, marked **Waiting to send**; a Pot
entry is marked **Not sent** and counted in the balances, which say *Includes entries not sent yet*. Under the banner
the app says how many changes wait, and the **Uploads** menu lists them, each with **Discard**. Everything else —
sharing, moving, restoring, a Memory's people or music, a Pot's settings — waits until you are online: *You're offline.
Changes are possible again once you're back online.*

Once the server can be reached, the app sends your changes in the order you made them and says how many were sent.
Nothing someone else changed meanwhile is overwritten: if they edited the same entry, or a name is taken now, your change
waits in **Uploads** — **Open** shows the entry with who changed it and when, your values in the form, to save or
**Discard mine**. A change that can no longer be made (the item is gone, you lost access, no storage left) says why; a
photo or file in it can be kept with **Save a copy** before you discard it.

Your changes wait on this device sealed, as kept content does. Signing out, **Stop trusting this device** or **Remove all
offline content** while changes wait asks first and names how many would be deleted. If the device was removed from your
account meanwhile, they are deleted without asking.

## Sync while the app is closed

On an Android phone or an iPhone, an account that stays signed in with **Stay signed in** — the device's own unlock
is enough, the app asks nothing at its start — also syncs while the app is closed: now and then the system starts the
app, which sends the changes you made offline, updates what you made available offline, and closes the account again.
It does nothing else, and it opens the account no more than starting the app would.

It is on from the start. To turn it off, go to **Storage on this device** in Settings and turn off **Sync in the
background**.

- Where the app asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock at each start (**Sign in with fingerprint or screen
  lock**, on an iPhone **Sign in with Face ID**), or where you sign in with your password, there is no switch, and
  Settings says why: *Syncing in the background is offered only where you stay signed in without confirming it is you:
  while the app is closed, nobody is there to confirm.*
- The system decides when it runs. On Android at most every 15 minutes, with a network and the battery not low. On an
  iPhone more rarely, and only while the phone is unlocked: a locked iPhone keeps the app's key closed, and the run ends
  having done nothing.
- On a computer the app syncs while it runs; it does not sync when it is closed.
- Your server sees the app's requests at times you did not open it ([What the server
  sees](/security/what-the-server-sees/)).

## When kept content goes

The app removes what it kept for an account:

- when you **sign out** of it on this device, choose **Stop trusting this device**, or remove the device;
- when you sign this device out from another one with **Sign out and remove saved key**, the next time this device
  reaches the server;
- when you choose **Remove all offline content**, or remove the app.

Your content stays on your server: sign in again and it is there.

When something is deleted, or you lose access to it, the app drops its copy the next time it is online. If it was
available offline, it says so: *"…" is no longer available offline.* A space your server has taken offline is not
shown either, once the app has heard so from the server: [Offline or unreachable](/help/offline/).

What a stolen device can still open, and why: [Known limitations](/security/known-limitations/#the-apps-offline-store).
