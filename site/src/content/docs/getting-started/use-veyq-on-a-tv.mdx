The veyq app also runs on **Apple TV** and on **Android TV / Google TV**. On the TV you **look** at what is in your
spaces: your Memories with their slideshows, music and videos, your Depot's photos, videos and music, your Pots, and
Search. You never change anything there, and nothing leaves the TV: there is no download, no export and no sharing.

:::caution[A preview, not released yet]
The TV app is a **preview**: no store lists it yet.
:::

## Sign in with your phone

A TV never asks for your password or your Secret Key. It shows a **QR code** instead:

1. On the TV, choose **Add someone** (or start the app the first time). The TV shows a code.
2. On your phone, open veyq, choose **Add a device** and scan the code
   ([Adding another device](/account/adding-another-device/)).
3. Type the **six digits** your phone shows into the TV with the remote.

## Your own PIN

A TV is shared, so each person keeps their account behind **their own six-digit PIN**:

- Right after you signed in, the TV asks you to choose a PIN, twice. If you skip it, the TV keeps nothing, and you sign
  in with your phone again next time.
- The TV then starts at a **picker**: everyone who keeps an account on this TV has a tile. Choose yours and type your
  PIN. Only your account opens; the others stay locked.
- **Ten wrong PINs in a row** remove your account from this TV. Sign it in again with your phone.
- The TV **locks again** when you choose **Lock**, whenever the app comes back from the background, and after
  **15 minutes** without a button press while nothing is playing.
- In the TV's settings you can **change your PIN**, change the **name** your tile shows, and **remove** your account
  from this TV.

If you forget your PIN, remove the account (or let ten tries remove it) and sign in again with your phone.

## What you see on a TV

- **Memories** — your Favorites, Recent and all Memories, a Memory's photos and chapters, the slideshow with the
  Memory's music, videos, and the music playing on. Not the map and not the journal.
- **Depot** — your folders, and photos, videos and music in the viewers. Any other file is listed and says it opens on
  another device.
- **Expenses** — a Pot's entries and overview, to read.
- **Search** — type with the TV's own keyboard or your voice; the results open what they find.

Received share links don't open on a TV, and the TV keeps no content for offline use.
