veyq is a place to keep photos, videos and files together with the people they belong to, such as your family,
your friends or a club. Everything is locked on your own device before it is sent anywhere, so the server that
stores it cannot look inside.

## Spaces

What you keep lives in **spaces**. You have more than one:

- **The shared space.** The one you were invited into, for example "Example Family". Every member sees what is in
  it.
- **Personal.** Your own space. Only you can open what is in it: not the other members, and not the person who
  runs the server.
- **Groups.** Smaller circles of members, each with a space of its own that only its members can open.

The sidebar on the left lists the shared space and **Personal** under **Spaces**, and your groups below them under
**Groups**.

## What you keep in a space

A space is organised in folders, like the files on your computer. What you put in them comes from an
[application](/applications/):

- **Memories**, for the photos and videos of an occasion: a trip, a wedding, a birthday.
- **Depot**, for documents, PDFs and any other file you want to keep.

## How you get in

You cannot sign up on your own: someone who owns a space invites you by email. Then:

1. [Accept the invitation](/getting-started/accept-an-invitation/) and create your account.
2. [Save your Emergency Kit](/getting-started/emergency-kit/). It is the one thing that gets you back in if
   you forget your password.
3. [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in/), on this device and on every new one.

Why the server cannot read what you keep, and what it does see: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
