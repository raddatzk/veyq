The veyq [app](/getting-started/install-the-app/) on **Android**, an **iPhone** or an **iPad** appears in your
device's share menu. Photos, videos and PDFs you share from another app, such as your gallery or your files, go into
a place in veyq that you choose. The app locks them before it sends them, as with anything you upload.

## Share from another app

1. In the other app, select the files, choose **Share**, then **veyq**.
2. veyq opens at your space's Home and says **Choose where these files go**. What cannot take the files is dimmed, and
   so is a place where you may not add anything.
3. Choose the place:
   - **A Memory or a Chapter** takes photos and videos. Open the Memory, or the Chapter inside it, and choose
     **Add here**. The files upload into it as if you had [added them](/memories/add-photos-and-videos/) there.
   - **A Pot** with people in it takes images and PDFs of up to 10 MB each. Choosing it opens a
     [new expense](/expenses/record-an-expense/) with the files as its receipts; saving the expense saves them.

Nothing is stored until you choose **Add here** or save the expense. Leave the Memory, or close the expense without
saving, and the files wait again: you can choose another place. **Cancel** ends the share. What a place does not
take is left out, and veyq says how many files that were. A Memory or a Chapter can be chosen only while the app
reaches your server.

A photo the other app offers as HEIC is sent as a JPEG, so every browser can show it.

### On Android

The app reads the shared files where they are and makes no copy of them, except a HEIC photo's JPEG, which it
removes once it is sent. After **Add here** you can leave the app: the upload goes on, and a notification says how
many files are still on their way.

### On an iPhone or iPad

veyq's share window opens inside the other app. It works with an account that stays signed in on this device, after
Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, or one that only asks for your password. Otherwise it says *Open veyq and sign in
first. Then you can share to it.*

- **Into a Pot**, you save the expense right there.
- **Into a Memory or a Chapter**, **Add here** closes the share window at once. The upload starts the next time you
  open veyq. Until then the files wait on your device in veyq's own storage, not yet locked: they are the files the
  other app already holds there. Each one is removed once it is uploaded. **Cancel** removes them at once, and
  signing out of veyq removes what still waits.

## Drop files onto the app

On Android, an iPad and a Mac you can also drag photos, videos and PDFs from another app onto veyq:

- **Onto a Memory's or a Chapter's gallery:** the photos and videos among them upload into it at once.
- **Onto an open Pot** with people in it: a new expense opens with them as its receipts.
- **Anywhere else:** veyq asks where they go, as for a share.

On Linux and Windows the app has no place in the share menu; drop photos and videos onto a Memory instead.
