When you create your account, veyq makes your **Emergency Kit**: a one-page PDF with your email address, your
**Secret Key** and a QR code that holds both. Keep it as carefully as a house key.

<Screenshot id="getting-started-kit-pdf" shows="The Emergency Kit PDF: the heading Emergency Kit, the email address, the Secret Key in three lines, three lines for addresses with the one it was created on in the first, and the QR code beside three steps" />

## What is on it

- **Your email and your Secret Key.** The Secret Key is nine groups of six letters and digits, such as
  `0C5126-0Z4RPK-…`. When you type it, capitals and the hyphens don't matter, and an O counts as a zero and an I or L
  as a one. Its last two characters are a check, so veyq notices nearly every typo and says so before it tries the
  key.
- **Where you sign in.** Three lines for the addresses you sign in at, the first already holding the address of the
  page you made the kit on. You can type more into the PDF or write them on the printout. The QR code carries that
  first address too, so veyq can tell you when you use the kit somewhere else.
- **A QR code** with your email and your Secret Key, and three steps for signing in with it. Your password is
  deliberately not on the kit.

## What you need it for

- **Signing in on a new device.** The first time on each device, veyq asks for your password **and** your Secret
  Key: [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in/). After that, that device usually doesn't ask for it again.
- **A forgotten password.** Resetting it takes a link we email you **and** the Secret Key from your kit. Neither
  works alone.
- **[Securing your account](/account/replace-your-keys/)** when someone else may have your password, your Secret
  Key or one of your devices, and changing the email address you sign in with.

## Keep it safe

- Print it, or save it where you keep other important papers, such as a password manager. Not only on the device you
  use veyq on: if that device is lost, the kit should not be lost with it.
- Don't share it. Someone who has your kit **and** your password can open everything you keep.
- veyq cannot show you your kit again later. You get a new one in two cases: after you change your email address
  (the old kit keeps working), and when you secure your account, which also gives you a new Secret Key (the old kit
  stops working).
- If someone else may have your kit, [secure your account](/account/replace-your-keys/). That needs your
  password and the kit you have now.

## If it is lost

As long as you know your password and are signed in on a device, nothing is lost: you keep using veyq as before. But
you cannot get a new kit, and without it you cannot reset a forgotten password or secure your account. Use that time to
[add a second device](/account/adding-another-device/), so one lost device does not end it:
[Lost Emergency Kit](/help/lost-emergency-kit/).

If you lose **both** your password and your Emergency Kit, nobody can bring back what you keep, not the people who
own your space and not the person who runs the server. There is no master key on their side and no back door. The
same thing that stops them from reading your photos stops them from rescuing them.

A server brought back from a backup changes nothing about this. If the person who runs the server restores it and you
still have your password and your Emergency Kit, everything you keep opens exactly as before: nothing was unlocked,
re-encrypted or handed to anyone on the way.

The Secret Key is long and completely random, so nobody can guess it. Because your data needs it as well as your
password, a guessed or leaked password alone opens nothing: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
