You get into veyq when someone who owns a space invites you, in one of two ways:

- **A link they send you themselves**, in a message or an email of their own. It opens the invitation by itself, once.
- **An email from veyq**, in English and German, with a link that works until the date it names. The person who
  invited you passes on a **code** to you themselves, by message or in person: this link alone does not open the
  invitation.

## Enter the code

1. Open the link. The page **Accept your invitation** says which space you have been invited to join. A link the
   person sent you themselves carries its code, so the page goes straight on to your account.
2. For an invitation from veyq's email, enter the **Code** the person who invited you passed on, and choose **Continue**. Capitals, spaces and the hyphen
   do not matter. If they chose a password for the invitation instead, the page asks for the **Password for the
   invitation**.

Your device then checks that the invitation really comes from the person who sent it, before anything leaves it.

## Create your account

3. Enter a **Username**. It is how the others in this space find you (letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`), and it
   must not be taken yet.
4. If you like, add your first and last name and a picture. Only members of the space can see them, and you can
   change them later in the settings.
5. Choose a **Password** of at least 10 characters. When you leave the field, veyq checks whether the password is
   known from data breaches; if it is, choose another one, or tick **Use it anyway**.
6. Choose **Continue**.

<Screenshot id="getting-started-accept-form" shows="The Accept your invitation page with the space's name, the Username and Password fields and the Continue button" />

For an invitation by email, your email address is the one it was sent to; you do not type it. With a link someone
sent you, enter your **Email** before the password; if you already have an account on this server, choose **I already
have an account** and sign in with it instead. If you were invited to **own** a new space, there is no code to enter,
and the last button below says **Accept & activate**.

## Save your Emergency Kit

7. Choose **Download Emergency Kit (PDF)** and keep the file somewhere safe. What it is and where to keep it:
   [Your Emergency Kit](/getting-started/emergency-kit/).
8. Tick **I have saved my Emergency Kit somewhere safe.**
9. Choose **Accept & join**.

<Screenshot id="getting-started-accept-kit" shows="The Save your Emergency Kit step with the Download Emergency Kit (PDF) button, the confirmation checkbox ticked and the Accept & join button" />

The page then says **You’ve joined “Example Family”.**, with your space's name. Choose **Sign in** and sign in with your
email, your password and the Secret Key from the kit you just saved: [Sign in](/getting-started/sign-in/).

If you joined with a new account through a link, the page also says a confirmation is on its way to your address.
Open the link in that mail while signed in. Until you do, every page shows that your email address is not confirmed,
with **Send confirmation mail**: a password-reset link goes to that address, so veyq makes sure early that it reaches
you ([Your email address](/account/change-email/)).

The shared space is yours to see right away: the invitation carried its key, locked under its code, and your device
took it over when you joined. Nobody else has to be online for that.

## If it does not work

- **This invitation is no longer valid.** It has expired, or was cancelled or sent again. Ask whoever invited you to
  send a new one.
- **This invitation link has already been used. Tell whoever invited you — they can send you a new one.** Someone
  opened it before you. If that was not you, tell them, so they know who else may have joined.
- **You are already a member of this space.** The account you signed in with is in it already.
- **This invitation link is invalid.** The link is incomplete. Open it again from where you got it.
- **That code does not open this invitation.** The code is mistyped, or it belongs to another invitation. Check it
  and try again.
- **This invitation can’t be checked — ask for a new one.** The invitation does not match the person who sent it, so
  your device sends nothing. When they send it again, you get a new link.
- **That email already has an account — sign in instead.** You already have a veyq account with this address on
  this server. The page asks for your password and your Secret Key instead of a new password; with a link someone
  sent you, choose **I already have an account**.

Your password and your Secret Key never leave your device. veyq turns them into keys on your device, and the server
only ever receives locked data: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
