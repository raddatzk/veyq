You get into veyq when someone who owns a space invites you. The invitation comes by email, in English and German,
with a link that works until the date it names. The person who invited you passes on a **code** to you themselves,
by message or in person: the link alone does not open the invitation.

## Enter the code

1. Open the link in the email. The page **Accept your invitation** says which space you have been invited to join.
2. Enter the **Code** the person who invited you passed on, and choose **Continue**. Capitals, spaces and the hyphen
   do not matter. If they chose a password for the invitation instead, the page asks for the **Password for the
   invitation**.

Your device then checks that the invitation really comes from the person who sent it, before anything leaves it.

## Create your account

3. Enter a **Username**. It is how the others in this space find you (letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`), and it
   must not be taken yet.
4. If you like, add your first and last name and a picture. Only members of the space can see them, and you can
   change them later in the settings.
5. Choose a **Password** of at least 10 characters. When you leave the field, veyq checks whether the password is
   known from data breaches; if it is, choose another one, or tick **Use it anyway**.
6. Choose **Continue**.

<Screenshot id="getting-started-accept-form" shows="The Accept your invitation page with the space's name, the Username and Password fields and the Continue button" />

Your email address is the one the invitation was sent to; you do not type it. If you were invited to **own** a new
space, there is no code to enter, and the last button below says **Accept & activate**.

## Save your Emergency Kit

7. Choose **Download Emergency Kit (PDF)** and keep the file somewhere safe. What it is and where to keep it:
   [Your Emergency Kit](/veyq/getting-started/emergency-kit/).
8. Tick **I have saved my Emergency Kit somewhere safe.**
9. Choose **Accept & join**.

<Screenshot id="getting-started-accept-kit" shows="The Save your Emergency Kit step with the Download Emergency Kit (PDF) button, the confirmation checkbox ticked and the Accept & join button" />

The page then says **You’ve joined “Example Family”.**, with your space's name. Choose **Sign in** and sign in with your
email, your password and the Secret Key from the kit you just saved: [Sign in](/veyq/getting-started/sign-in/).

The shared space is yours to see right away: the invitation carried its key, locked under the code, and your device
took it over when you joined. Nobody else has to be online for that.

## If it does not work

- **This invitation is no longer valid.** It has expired, was cancelled or sent again, or was already used. Ask
  whoever invited you to send a new one.
- **This invitation link is invalid.** The link is incomplete. Open it again from the email.
- **That code does not open this invitation.** The code is mistyped, or it belongs to another invitation. Check it
  and try again.
- **This invitation can’t be checked — ask for a new one.** The invitation does not match the person who sent it, or
  they have secured their account since they sent it, so your device sends nothing. When they send it again, you get a new link and they get a new code to pass on.
- **That email already has an account — sign in instead.** You already have a veyq account with this address on
  this server. After the code, the page asks for your password and your Secret Key instead of a new password.

Your password and your Secret Key never leave your device. veyq turns them into keys on your device, and the server
only ever receives locked data: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
