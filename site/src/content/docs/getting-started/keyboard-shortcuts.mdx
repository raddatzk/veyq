Press <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere in veyq to see which keys do what. The same overview is in the menu at the bottom of the
sidebar, under **Keyboard shortcuts**. <kbd>Esc</kbd>, the × or a click beside it closes it again.

While you type in a field, <kbd>?</kbd> is just a question mark. While another window is open over the page, such as a
photo shown large, <kbd>?</kbd> does nothing; close it first.

The overview lists the shortcuts in groups:

- **Everywhere**: <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) goes to the
  [search](/spaces/search/), and <kbd>?</kbd> shows the overview.
- **In the search**: <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> move through the list, <kbd>Enter</kbd> chooses the highlighted entry,
  <kbd>Backspace</kbd> in an empty field removes the last filter, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the list.
- **In the gallery** of a Memory: the arrow keys move between the photos and videos, <kbd>Home</kbd> and
  <kbd>End</kbd> go to the first and the last, <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> one screen up or down.
- **In the large view** of a photo: <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> go to the previous or the next, <kbd>+</kbd> and
  <kbd>-</kbd> zoom in and out, <kbd>0</kbd> shows the whole picture again, and while zoomed in the arrow keys move the
  picture. <kbd>Delete</kbd> or <kbd>Backspace</kbd> (<kbd>⌫</kbd> on a Mac) deletes the photo, where you may - a member moves it to the trash. <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes
  the large view; zoomed in, it first shows the whole picture again.

The overview names the keys of your device: <kbd>⌘</kbd> on a Mac, an iPhone or an iPad, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> elsewhere. An
application that is switched off in your space has no group there.
