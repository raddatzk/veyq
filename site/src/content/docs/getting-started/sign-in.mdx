The first time you sign in on a device, veyq asks for three things: your email, your password and the Secret Key
from your [Emergency Kit](/getting-started/emergency-kit/). After that, the device asks for less: how much
depends on one choice you make while signing in.

## Sign in on a new device

1. Open your space's address. The page **Sign in** opens.
2. Enter your **Email** and your **Password**.
3. Give veyq your **Secret Key**, in whichever way is easiest:
   - **Type or paste it** into the field.
   - Choose **Upload Emergency Kit** (the upload arrow in the field) and pick the kit's PDF, or a photo or screenshot
     of its QR code. A kit you saved again, printed to PDF, or photographed or scanned into a PDF works too. This
     fills in your email too.
   - Choose **Scan QR code** (the QR-code symbol in the field) and hold the kit's QR code up to your camera. This
     button only appears where the browser can use a camera.
4. Decide on **Stay signed in on this device**: on, this device opens straight to your space next time; off, it asks
   for your email and password only.
5. Choose **Sign in**.

Use **Stay signed in on this device** only on a device that is yours. On someone else's computer, sign in in a
private window and sign out when you are done: an ordinary window keeps a saved key here that, together with your
password, opens your space without the Secret Key.

## On a device you have used before

- **Stay signed in on this device was on:** the space opens without asking anything. Once you sign out, though, the
  next sign-in here asks for your Secret Key again.
- **It was off:** your email is already filled in and there is no Secret Key field: your password is enough. If
  you sign in here with more than one account, the email stays empty, and the Secret Key field disappears as soon as
  you type yours.

## Without your Emergency Kit at hand

- **Sign in with a QR code** lets a device you are already signed in on vouch for the new one:
  [Add another device](/account/adding-another-device/).
- **I don't have my Secret Key with me** signs you in without opening what you keep, so that you can sign your other
  devices out, for example after losing one.
- **Forgot your password?** starts a reset. It needs your Secret Key as well.

## What you see first

If your space has terms, veyq shows them first: read them and choose **I agree**. Then the shared space opens. If
the space got a new lock that has not reached you yet, you start in **Personal**, and the shared space is marked
**Waiting for the current key** until another member's device hands it over.

## If it does not work

- **Sign-in failed. Check your email and password.** The email or the password is wrong.
  veyq does not say which of the two: a wrong password and an email with no account look exactly the same, so nobody
  can use the sign-in page to find out who has an account here.
- **Unlock failed. Check your password and Secret Key.** The Secret Key does not belong to this account, or has a
  typo.
- **This Secret Key has a typo.** The key you typed has the right length, but its last two characters, the check, do
  not match the rest. Compare it with your kit group by group.
- **Couldn't read that kit.** The file is not your Emergency Kit, or the QR code is not readable in it. Try the PDF
  itself, or type the Secret Key.
- **This page and this server don't match.** veyq checks this before you sign in, so nothing fails halfway through. If
  the page is older, reload it; if the server is older, the person who runs it has to update it. Your account and your
  data are fine.

Your password and your Secret Key never leave this device. The server never gets anything it could use to open what
you keep: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)

Signing in works with a one-time signature that your device makes from your password. The server only checks it, and
keeps nothing it could reuse to sign in as you.
