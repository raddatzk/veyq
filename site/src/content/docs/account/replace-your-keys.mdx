For when someone else may have your password, your Secret Key, or a device that was signed in to your account.

## What gets replaced

Your **Master Key**, your **identity** and your **Secret Key**. So you get a new **Emergency Kit**, and your old kit
stops working once your account is secured. You can choose a new password at the same time; it is offered and
pre-selected, and we recommend it.

## Why

Signing a device out ends its access to the server, not the keys it holds. New keys shut those out of everything
added from now on. A new Secret Key also shuts out someone who copied your Emergency Kit.

## Do you need this?

Only if someone else may have your password, your Secret Key or a device that was signed in to your account. For a
lost device of your own, choose **Sign out and remove this device's saved key** instead
([Devices and sessions](/veyq/account/devices-and-sessions/)).

## What happens

- **You keep every space you are in.** The people there see that your account was secured; nobody has to confirm
  anything. Their apps accept your new identity because your old one signed it: your profile shows them **New
  identity, signed by their previous key** with the date.
- **All your devices are signed out.** Each signs in again with your password and your **new** Emergency Kit, or you
  add it from this one with **Add a device**. The device you secure your account on stays unlocked, even after you
  reload the page.
- **You get a new Emergency Kit.** You save it before anything changes, and your old kit stops working once your
  account is secured.
- **Invitations you sent keep working.** Your app signs them again with your new identity, so the people you invited
  can still join, and nothing changes for the people who joined through you.
- **It can't be undone.** What someone else already saw stays seen. Only what is added afterwards is closed to them.
- **Links you made before stay open until you renew them or switch them off.** You see a list at the end
  ([Renew a link](/veyq/sharing/manage-links/)).

Before the fields, the page lists any space that can't get its new lock yet, and why: it is paused, it is being
deleted, or this device can't open it, so it is already closed to you.

You need your current **Emergency Kit** to start. Without it, your account can't be secured.

## Secure it

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Secure your account**.
2. Enter **Your password** and **Your Secret Key**, the one from your current Emergency Kit.
3. **Also choose a new password** is already ticked. Enter **New password** and **Confirm new password**, or untick it
   to keep yours.
4. Choose **Continue**. If the password or Secret Key doesn't match, you stay on this step and nothing has changed.
5. Under **Your new Emergency Kit**, choose **Download Emergency Kit (PDF)** and keep the file where only you can reach
   it. Keep your old kit until the page says your account is secured.
6. Tick **I have saved my Emergency Kit somewhere safe.** The button stays off until you have.
7. Choose **Secure your account** and confirm with **Secure your account** in the dialog.

The page then says **Your account is secured. Your old Emergency Kit no longer works, so you can destroy it.** Under
**Your spaces** each space shows its state:

- **New lock.** What is added here from now on is closed to the old key. Links made before stay open to it until
  you renew them or switch them off.
- **Fitting the new lock.** The new lock is going in. Adding something here takes a moment longer until it's done.
- **Not yet.** The new lock goes in the next time one of your devices opens veyq. What is here stays yours.
- **Not possible yet.** The space is paused or being deleted. A paused space gets its new lock once it is back.

Under **Links to renew**, each link you made before is listed with its space. **Renew** opens that link's own share
dialog. An upload link isn't renewed: make a new one, then switch the old one off. A link in another shared space says which one
it is in; open veyq there to renew it.

## If something goes wrong

- **The page says it couldn't confirm whether your account was secured.** Unlock again with your **new** Emergency
  Kit. If it opens, your account is secured and veyq finishes the rest without a new kit. If it is refused, nothing was
  changed and your old kit still works.
- **The page says the request is larger than this server accepts.** Nothing was changed, and trying again won't help.
  Ask whoever runs your server to raise its request limit.
- **You closed the page before the end.** Sign in with your password and your **new** kit. If it opens, veyq
  finishes the rest by itself. If it is refused, nothing was changed and your old kit still works.
- **You start Secure your account again with the Secret Key from your new kit.** Your account was already secured, so
  veyq finishes what was left, with no new kit.

When another member of a space you are in secures their account, their own device fits the new lock there, and every
member's device helps put it in. Adding something there takes a moment longer while it goes in. Nobody has to confirm
anything.
