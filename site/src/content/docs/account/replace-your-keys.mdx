For an emergency: someone else has your keys, for example through a device you approved that wasn't yours.

## What gets replaced

Your **Master Key** and your **identity**. Your password, Secret Key and Emergency Kit stay as they are.

## Why

Signing a device out ends its access to the server, not the keys it holds. New keys shut those out of everything
added from now on.

## Do you need this?

Only if a device that isn't yours had your keys. For a lost device of your own, choose **Sign out and remove this
device's saved key** instead ([Devices and sessions](/veyq/account/devices-and-sessions/)).

## What it costs

- **Everyone sees that your key changed.** Whoever had verified you compares the safety number again.
- **You are outside every shared space until someone there confirms your new key**
  ([Verify a person](/veyq/account/verify-a-person/)).
- **Every other device is signed out** and needs your password and Secret Key again, or to be added from this one
  with **Add a device**.
- **Open group invitations to you lapse.**
- **It can't be undone.** What is there now stays readable to the old key; only what is added afterwards is closed
  to it.
- **Links made before stay open to the old key until you renew them**
  ([Renew a link](/veyq/sharing/manage-links/)).

Two things before you start:

- **If you are the only owner of a shared space, the page lists it by name.** Make someone else an owner there
  first, or that space keeps the old lock.
- **You need your Emergency Kit.** Without it, your keys can't be replaced. Keep it where only you can reach it.

## Replace them

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Security**.
2. Under **Replace your keys**, enter **Your password** and **Your Secret Key**.
3. Choose **Replace my keys** and confirm with **Replace my keys** in the dialog.

If the page says the request is larger than this server accepts, nothing was changed and trying again won't help.
Ask whoever runs your server to raise its request limit.

The page then says **Your keys are replaced** and lists each space with its state:

- **New lock.** What is added here from now on is closed to the old key. Links made before stay open to it until
  you renew them.
- **Fitting the new lock.** The new lock is going in. Adding something here takes a moment longer until it's done.
- **Old lock.** This space still has its old lock. Try again.

Under **Shared spaces**, someone else fits the new lock and lets you back in:

- **Waiting for another owner.** Another owner fits the new lock the next time they open veyq. Until then, the old
  key reads what is added here.
- **Nobody can do it.** No other owner is left who can fit the new lock. Make someone an owner here. Until then, the
  old key reads what is added here.
- **Fitting the new lock.** The new lock is going in. Someone here still has to confirm it's you.
- **New lock.** New lock in. Someone here still has to confirm it's you.
- **Old lock.** Fitting the new lock kept failing, so the old key still reads what is added here. Start it again.

If you own a shared space or a group and another member replaced their keys, one owner's device, perhaps yours, starts
fitting the new lock by itself, and every member's device helps put it in; adding something there takes a moment
longer while it goes in. If that cannot happen, the space shows **Needs a
new lock** in the sidebar, and its member list says why. Either no owner is left who can fit it: make someone else
an owner. Or fitting it kept failing: choose **Start again**, and your device takes it on.
