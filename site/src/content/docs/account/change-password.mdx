You need your current password and the Secret Key from your [Emergency Kit](/getting-started/emergency-kit/).
If you have forgotten your password, [reset it](/account/forgot-password/) instead.

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Change password**.
2. Enter **Your current password**.
3. Enter a **New password** of at least 10 characters, and the same again under **Confirm new password**. When you
   leave the field, veyq checks whether the password is known from data breaches; if it is, choose another one, or
   tick **Use it anyway**.
4. Enter **Your Secret Key**: type or paste it, or use the upload or scan button in the field.
5. Choose **Change password**.

<Screenshot id="account-change-password" shows="The Change password panel with the current password, the new password twice and the Secret Key filled in, and the Change password button" />

The panel then says **Your password is changed.** Every other device is signed out and loses its saved key: to get
back in, each one needs the new password and your Secret Key, or you add it again from this device with
[Add a device](/account/adding-another-device/). This device loses its saved key too; choose **Remember this
device** if you want it to stay signed in.

Your Secret Key, your Emergency Kit and everything you keep stay as they are.

## If it does not work

- **Those two passwords don't match.** Type the new password the same way twice.
- **That current password didn't match. Nothing was changed.** Check your current password.
- **That current password and Secret Key don't open this account.** Check the Secret Key against your kit.

Your new password never leaves this device, just as the old one never did: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
