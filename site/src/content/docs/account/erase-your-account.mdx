You can leave this instance for good. Your account and what is kept about you are erased, apart from what the law
keeps, and you are told what that is before you confirm.

## Before you start

- **Delete from shared spaces what you want gone.** What you put into a space or group you share with others stays
  there after you leave and shows as a former member's: it belongs to that space, like a guest's photos belong to the
  Memory. Delete it while you are still a member.
- **If you are the last owner of a shared space**, decide what happens to it: you can delete it along with your account,
  or make one of its members the owner.

## Erase it

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Erase account**.
2. Read what goes and what stays.
3. Under **Spaces you are the last owner of**, choose for each space either **Delete the space** or **Make … the owner**.
   Spaces where you are the only member are listed under **Deleted along with your account**.
4. Enter **Your password** and choose **Erase account**, then confirm.

You are signed out on every device at once, and you cannot sign in again. This device forgets what it kept for your
account: the saved session, the remembered and trusted device, and the marks it keeps about your account.

## What happens next

- **For 30 days** your account waits, unless whoever runs your server set another period. During that time only they
  can stop the erasure, for example if somebody else erased it. You get an email that says so and names the date. If
  you did not ask for it, contact them at once.
- **Then** your account is erased: its keys, devices and sessions, your profiles and memberships in every space, your
  favorites, your personal space with everything in it, and your address. A space you chose to delete goes on the same
  date.
- **What stays**: what you put into shared spaces, as a former member's. Reports, moderation records and held copies
  that name your account stay until their legal retention ends, and with them the record of which terms you accepted
  while such a case is open. Backups of this instance keep your account for at most as many days as the operator
  set (30 unless they chose otherwise), and if one is ever restored, your account is erased again before the instance
  answers anyone.

If the erasure is stopped, you can sign in again. Your personal space is in the trash until its 30 days end, and the
shared spaces you left need a new invitation.

## If it does not work

- **That password didn’t match. Nothing was erased.**
- **Choose what happens to every space you are the last owner of. Nothing was erased.**
- **You hold the moderation key alone.** Another moderator must hold it before you can go.
- **This is the Server Owner’s account.** It cannot be erased: nobody would be left to run this instance.

Whoever runs your server can also erase your account for you, if you cannot sign in any more. Nothing is decrypted to
erase it: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
