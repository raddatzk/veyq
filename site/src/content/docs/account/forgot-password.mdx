Resetting your password takes two things: a link we email you, and the Secret Key from your
[Emergency Kit](/getting-started/emergency-kit/). Neither works alone.

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot your password?**.
2. Enter **Your email address** and choose **Send me a link**.
3. Open the link in the email (it works for one hour). Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.
4. Under **Enter your Secret Key**, type, paste or upload your Secret Key and choose **Continue**.
5. Under **Choose a new password**, enter a **New password** of at least 10 characters, then **Repeat the new
   password**, and choose **Reset password**.
6. The page says **Done. Sign in with your new password.** and takes you to the sign-in page.

<Screenshot id="account-recover-key" shows="The Reset your password page at the step Enter your Secret Key, with the account's email address and the Secret Key field" />

Everything you keep is still there, and your Emergency Kit keeps working: your Secret Key does not change. Every
device signs in fresh afterwards, so you enter your Secret Key once more on each, this one included.

## If it does not work

- **This space cannot send email yet, so no link can go out.** Ask whoever owns your space to set up email in its
  settings. Your account is fine, and the reset works as soon as they have.
- **That Secret Key did not open your account.** Check it against your kit; the link still works.
- **This link no longer works.** It expired after an hour or was already used. Ask for a new one.

If your Emergency Kit is gone too, nobody can bring your data back: there is no master key on the server and no back
door. Someone who breaks into your mailbox gets a link, but no way to open your account without your kit:
[Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
