Your email address is what you sign in with, and where a link goes if you ever
[forget your password](/account/forgot-password/). Both tasks below are under **Settings → Secure your account**.

## Check that a reset link would reach you

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Secure your account**.
2. Under **Email address** you see your address and whether it is confirmed.
3. If it says **Not confirmed yet**, choose **Send confirmation**.
4. Open the link in that email on a device where you are signed in to veyq. The page says **Your email address is
   confirmed.**

If you joined through an emailed invitation, your address is usually confirmed already; if you joined through a link,
it is not. While it is not confirmed, every page shows a note naming your address, with **Send confirmation mail**,
which does the same as step 3; the note goes once you have opened the link. It is worth doing once: then you know a
reset link reaches you before the day you need one.

## Change the address you sign in with

You need your password and the Secret Key from your [Emergency Kit](/getting-started/emergency-kit/).

1. Under **Settings → Secure your account**, choose **Change sign-in address**.
2. Enter the **New email address**, your **Password** and your **Secret Key** (type it, paste it, or import the kit).
3. Choose **Send link to the new address**.
4. Open the link in the email that arrives at the new address, on a device where you are signed in to this account.
   The page says **Done — you sign in with your new address from now on.**
5. On the same page, under **Print a new Emergency Kit**, enter your password and Secret Key (or import the old kit),
   choose **Check and make the kit**, then **Download Emergency Kit (PDF)**. Keep the new kit safe and tick **I have
   saved my Emergency Kit somewhere safe.**

<Screenshot id="account-change-email" shows="The Security settings with the Email address block and the Change sign-in address form: new email address, password, Secret Key and the Send link to the new address button" />

Until you open the link, nothing changes and you keep signing in with the old address. Afterwards your other devices
are signed out, your old address gets a short notice, and invitations sent to your old address can no longer be
accepted with this account. Your data, your keys and your Secret Key stay the same; the old kit keeps working, it
just shows the old address.

If the new address cannot be used, for example because another account already signs in with it, you only learn
that when you open the link. That is on purpose: otherwise anybody could use this form to find out which addresses
have an account.

## If it does not work

- **This space can't send email yet.** Whoever owns your space has not set up email. Ask them; nothing is wrong with
  your account.
- **That password or Secret Key didn't match. Nothing was changed.**
- **Sign in on this device first, then open the link from the email again.** The link only works on a device where
  you are signed in.

Your address is the one thing the server must know to send you mail; your password and Secret Key are checked on
this device and never sent: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
