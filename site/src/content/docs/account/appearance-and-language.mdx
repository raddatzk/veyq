1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Appearance and language**.
2. Under **Appearance**, choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
3. Under **Language**, choose **System**, **English** or **Deutsch**.

<Screenshot id="account-appearance" shows="The Appearance and language panel with the Appearance choice System, Light, Dark and the Language choice System, English, Deutsch" />

The same choices are in the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, under **Appearance** and **Language**. **System**
follows what your device is set to.

The choice applies to this browser on this device only. It is not sent to the server and does not follow you to
your other devices: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
