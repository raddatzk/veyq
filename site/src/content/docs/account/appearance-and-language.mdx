1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Appearance and language**.
2. Under **Appearance**, choose **System**, **Light** or **Dark**.
3. Under **Language**, choose **System**, **English** or **Deutsch**.

The same choices are in the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, under **Appearance** and **Language**. **System**
follows what your device is set to.

## In the app

Open **Settings** and go to **Appearance and language**. It offers the same choices, and each app keeps them for
itself:

- **Android:** choose under **Appearance** and **Language**; veyq changes at once. The language can also be set in
  your phone's settings for veyq — both are the same setting. On Android 12 there is no language choice: veyq speaks
  your phone's language.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** choose under **Appearance**; veyq changes at once. The language is chosen in your device's
  own settings for veyq: **Choose in Settings** (on a Mac **Choose in System Settings**) takes you there.
- **Linux:** the appearance changes at once, the language once veyq starts again.
- **Windows:** both change once veyq starts again.

A TV follows the TV's own appearance and language.

The choice applies to this browser, or this app, on this device only. It is not sent to the server and does not
follow you to your other devices: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
