Your profile is how the other members of a shared space see you: your username, and if you like your real name and a
picture. Each shared space has its own profile.

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Settings**, then **Your profile**.
2. Change your **Username** if you like (letters, digits, `.`, `_` and `-`; it must be free in this space).
3. Enter your **First name** and **Last name**, or leave them empty.
4. For a picture, choose **Choose a picture**, pick an image, move and zoom it into the square, and choose **Use
   this picture**. **Remove picture** takes it away again.
5. Choose **Save profile**.

<Screenshot id="account-profile" shows="Your profile with the username, the profile picture and its buttons, first and last name, the Ask before adding me to groups checkbox and the Save profile button" />

Your name and picture show where veyq shows a person: as who was there in a Memory, as who added a file or photo,
and on your profile page. Where you have no name, your username is shown. People you share a link with never see your
picture.

**Ask before adding me to groups**: when it is on, being added to a group becomes an invitation you accept or
decline. It applies in every space you belong to.

You can also edit name and picture on your own profile page, with **Edit profile**: choose your own name where veyq
shows it, for example on a file or photo you added. Right after joining a space, veyq may ask once with **Finish your
profile**; **Open profile settings** takes you here, **Not now** hides the notice. It waits while your email address still needs confirming.

If the page says your name or picture **can be added once your access to the shared space is ready**, another
member's device has not handed you the space's key yet. That happens by itself.

Your name and picture are locked with the space's key on your device before they leave it, so only its members can
see them, not the person who runs the server: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
