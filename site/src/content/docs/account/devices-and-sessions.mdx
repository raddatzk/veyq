Here you see every place your account is signed in, how this device unlocks, and you can sign any of them out.

## Open the list

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, the one with your picture and email address, and choose **Settings**.
2. Choose **Devices and sessions**.

Each row is one device. It shows the device's name, when it was last used and since when it is signed in. A new
device starts with a name like "Chrome · macOS"; a device without a name shows **Signed-in device**. Your own row
says **You are here**.

<Screenshot id="account-sessions-list" shows="Devices and sessions with two rows: this device with You are here and its unlock line, and another device with the pencil, the sign-out icon and Sign out + remove key" />

Your row also says how this device unlocks:

- **This device stays signed in** — you chose Stay signed in on this device; it opens without a password.
- **This device is trusted** — it unlocks with just your password.
- **This device has no saved unlock** — it needs your Secret Key every time.

## Give a device a name

1. Choose the pencil on its row (**Rename this device**).
2. Type a name, for example "Work laptop", and choose **Save**.

The name is stored locked; the server never sees it. Every session from that device shows it.

## Sign a device out

- **One other device:** choose the sign-out icon on its row (**Sign this device out**) and confirm. It has to sign
  in again.
- **Every other device at once:** choose **Sign out everywhere else** at the top and confirm. You stay signed in here.
- **This device:** the sign-out icon on your own row (**Sign out here**), or **Sign out** in the menu at the bottom
  of the sidebar.
  Every other veyq tab in this browser signs out with it.

A device that was set to stay signed in loses its saved key when it is signed out, and then needs your password
**and** your Secret Key.

Signing out closes everything veyq had open on this device: your keys and everything it unlocked are cleared from the
page. A device you sign out from elsewhere does the same once the server turns it away; a key it saved stays until you
remove it, as described below.

## If a device is really gone

Signing a device out cuts it off from the server, but it does not take back what it can open: a device that saved a
key can still open your data with your password. For a lost or stolen device, choose **Sign out + remove key** on
its row instead and confirm. Whoever holds it then needs your password **and** your Secret Key.

Devices that are signed out but still have a saved key on the server are listed under **Devices with a saved key**.
Remove one with the key icon (**Remove this saved key**), or all of them with **Remove every saved key except this
device's**. Your other devices then need your password and your Emergency Kit to get back in.

If the lost device was your only one, sign in somewhere else and use
[I don't have my Secret Key with me](#without-your-secret-key) to sign every device out.

## Make this device forget its key

On your own row, choose **Untrust this device** and confirm (**Forget this device?**). Your next unlock here will need
your Secret Key again. You stay signed in, and your other devices are not affected.

## Without your Secret Key

On the sign-in page of a borrowed device, enter your email and password and choose **I don't have my Secret Key with
me**. You are signed in, but nothing is opened, and the device keeps nothing. Choose **Sign out everywhere, including
here** to sign every device out. If the kit turns up after all, **I have my Secret Key after all** takes you back.

Device names are locked on your device, and signing out never reaches into a device itself; it only takes away what
the server keeps for it: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
