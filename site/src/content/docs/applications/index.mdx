An **application** is one kind of sealed thing you can keep in your spaces. veyq has three:

| | **Memories** | **Depot** | **Expenses** |
|---|---|---|---|
| For | Photos, videos and the occasions they belong to: a trip, a wedding, a birthday | Documents, PDFs and any other file you want to keep | Shared costs: a trip, a shared flat |
| You keep | Memories, with chapters | Files, and drop boxes that collect files from others | Pots: who paid what, and who owes whom |
| Start here | [Memories](/veyq/memories/) | [Depot](/veyq/depot/) | [Expenses](/veyq/expenses/) |

All three live side by side in the same folders of your space. A folder can hold a Memory, a file and a drop box at
the same time, and the same things work on all of them: you can rename, move, copy and delete them, give them a cover,
mark them as favorites and [share](/veyq/sharing/) them. A Pot sits beside them too; you can move, copy, download,
delete and share it like the others.

<Screenshot id="applications-one-folder" shows="A folder that holds a Memory, a file and a drop box together, in list view, with the tabs All, Memories, Depot and Drop boxes above the list" />

The tabs above the list — **All**, then one per kind, such as **Memories**, **Depot** and **Drop boxes** — show just
one kind at a time. Folders stay
visible in every tab, so you can keep moving through your space.

Whoever runs your space, or the server, can [switch an application off](/veyq/space-owners/capabilities/). Then a
banner at the top says so, nothing in it can be added or changed, and everything in it can still be opened,
downloaded, deleted and restored, so you have time to save what you want to keep.

Everything you keep in any application is locked on your device before it leaves it. What the server can and
cannot see: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
