Open the Pot and choose **Overview**. Each person's balance is what they paid and received against their share of
every expense and refund, with transfers counted in. It shows who is owed money and who owes it, and the row says how
it comes about: what the person paid, what they consumed, and what they transferred or received.

Above the balances, **You are** marks your own row. Choose yourself once; the choice is kept
on this device only. If you are in the Pot as a member of the space, you are already chosen until you choose otherwise.

## Settle up

Below the balances, the Pot suggests a short list of transfers that brings everyone to zero: who pays whom, and how
much. Every device shows the same suggestion.

<Screenshot id="expenses-balances-settle-up" shows="The Overview tab of a Pot with three people, each row with what they paid and consumed, one owed and two owing, and below it the suggested transfers, each with who pays whom and the amount" />

When a suggested transfer has been paid, record it from there. It is added as a transfer entry, and the balances
change with it. When everyone is at zero, the Pot says that nobody owes anything.

A person who was deactivated or deleted still counts. They stay in the balances while they paid or consumed anything,
a deactivated person by name, a deleted one as **deleted people**. If an old entry is changed so they owe or are owed
again, they are in the suggestion too.

## Where the money went

Below the suggestion, **Overview** shows the total per category. Per person, what they paid beside what they consumed
is in their row of the balances: looking at only one of the two can mislead. Refunds count against their category; transfers are not costs.

If the Pot says changes are missing, its figures are computed from what is there and may be wrong. See
[If changes are missing](/veyq/expenses/#if-changes-are-missing).
