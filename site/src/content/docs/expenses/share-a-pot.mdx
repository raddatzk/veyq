Friends on a trip do not need an account to follow the Pot. A member of the space can
[share](/veyq/sharing/create-a-link/) it with a link, and chooses what people with the link may do:

- Viewing: they see the entries, their history, the deleted entries, the balances, the settle-up suggestion and the
  overview.
- Viewing and adding: they can also add, change, delete and restore entries, and add and rename people.

There is no link that only lets people send something in, as a [drop box](/veyq/depot/drop-boxes/) has.

<Screenshot id="expenses-share-dialog" shows="The share dialog of a Pot with the choice between viewing only and viewing and adding entries" />

## What people with the link see

The same Pot page as you, with fewer functions:

- No **Settings**.
- They cannot deactivate or delete a person.
- An entry's history shows each version and when, but not who saved it.

Anyone with a link that may add can change or delete any entry, also one you wrote. What they change keeps your
version in the history, and what they delete goes to **Deleted entries**, where you can restore it. In the history
you see the name they gave when they opened the link.
