Open the Pot and choose **Add entry**. An entry is one of three kinds:

- **Expense**: someone paid, for themselves and others.
- **Refund**: money came back, such as a credit for a delayed flight.
- **Transfer**: one person paid another back.

## An expense or a refund

1. Type a title, the date and the currency, and choose a category.
2. Say who paid, with how much each. One bill can have several payers; the entry's amount is what they paid together.
   For a refund, say who received the money, and how much each.
3. Say how it is split. With nothing more, the whole amount is split equally among everyone in the Pot, the usual
   case: the editor shows it as one line, such as "All 3 people, equally". To split it another way, choose **Adjust**:
   - **By shares**, such as two shares for a couple and one for a single person; **Split equally among all** puts
     everyone back at one share. **By percent**, with up to two decimals and 100 % together. **By amount**, an exact
     amount for each person, adding up to what is split.
   - Each person is a row: pick who it is, type their value, and take off whoever is not part of it. **Add another
     person** adds a row.
   - To split part of the bill another way, add **items**. Each has a name, an amount and a split of its own. What the
     bill has beyond the items, such as a tip or what everyone shared, is **the rest**, with a split of its own.
4. Save.

An entry that does not add up cannot be saved, for example when the items come to more than the amount (the editor
says by how much) or the percents or amounts of a split do not make what they must. The editor says what is off, in red
where it is. Once the entry adds up,
**Shares** below it shows what each person's share comes to in the Pot's base currency, exactly as it will be saved.

## Receipts

Under **Receipts** in the entry, choose **Add receipt** to keep a photo of the bill or a PDF with it, each at most
10 MB, up to 100 per entry. They are uploaded when you save. Open a receipt to see it in the entry; one that cannot
be shown there can only be downloaded. To remove one, remove it and save. Everyone who sees the Pot can open
its receipts.

## A transfer

Choose who paid whom and the amount. A transfer has no category. To pay back what [Settle up](/expenses/settle-up/)
suggests, you can record it from there.

## Another currency

If you paid in a currency other than the Pot's base currency, the entry needs the rate to the base currency. Where
your server and space allow it, the rate fills in from the day's euro reference rates of the European Central Bank
(ECB), and the editor names their date. Your device fetches those rates itself, once a day; the request carries only
the date. You can always type another rate, and where no rate fills in, you type it. The entry keeps the rate it was
saved with, whatever the rates do later. **Shares** then also shows what each payer paid, converted with that rate.

## Change or delete an entry

Open the entry to change it. Every change keeps the earlier version: the entry shows **edited**, and its history lists
each version and when it was saved. Members of the space also see who saved each one.

If someone else changed the entry while you were editing, saving shows you their version. Save again to put yours on
top of it.

Deleting an entry moves it to the Pot's **Deleted entries**. It leaves the balances, and you can restore it from there
with its receipts until they are erased after the trash's retention time. After that it stays in the history only.
