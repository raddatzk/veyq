Open the Pot and choose **Add entry**. An entry is one of three kinds:

- **Expense**: someone paid, for themselves and others.
- **Refund**: money came back, such as a credit for a delayed flight.
- **Transfer**: one person paid another back.

## An expense or a refund

1. Type a title, the date, the amount and its currency, and choose a category.
2. Say who paid, with how much each. One bill can have several payers. For a refund, say who received the money.
3. Say how it is split:
   - **Equally** among the people you choose.
   - **By shares**, such as two shares for a couple and one for a single person.
   - **By exact amounts** per person.
   - **As line items**: each item with its amount and the people it belongs to, such as one bottle of wine for three.
     What the bill has beyond the items, like a tip, is spread in proportion to each person's items, or equally among
     the people on the bill.
4. Save.

<Screenshot id="expenses-entry-editor" shows="The entry editor for an expense split as line items: title, date, amount, currency and category at the top, the payer with their amount, three items each with its people, and the tip spread in proportion" />

An entry that does not add up cannot be saved, for example when the payers' amounts are not the total or the items
come to more than it. The editor says what is off.

## A transfer

Choose who paid whom and the amount. A transfer has no category. To pay back what [Settle up](/veyq/expenses/settle-up/)
suggests, you can record it from there.

## Another currency

If you paid in a currency other than the Pot's base currency, type the rate to the base currency. The entry keeps the
rate it was saved with.

## Change or delete an entry

Open the entry to change it. Every change keeps the earlier version: the entry shows **edited**, and its history lists
each version and when it was saved. Members of the space also see who saved each one.

If someone else changed the entry while you were editing, saving shows you their version. Save again to put yours on
top of it.

Deleting an entry moves it to the Pot's **Deleted entries**. It leaves the balances, and you can restore it from there.
