**Expenses** keeps **Pots** for shared costs: a trip, a shared flat, a club evening. Everyone puts in what they paid,
and the Pot shows at any time who paid what and who owes whom.

<Screenshot id="expenses-pot-entries" shows="An opened Pot on its Entries tab, with the tabs Entries, Balances, Overview, People and Settings, and a few entries, each with its title, date, amount and who paid" />

## What you can do

- [Record an expense](/veyq/expenses/record-an-expense/)
- [Settle up](/veyq/expenses/settle-up/)
- [Share a Pot](/veyq/expenses/share-a-pot/)

## Create a Pot

1. Open the folder where the Pot should be.
2. Choose **New**, then **New Pot**.
3. Type a name, choose the **base currency** (what every share is counted in), and type the names of the people in it.
4. Create it. The Pot opens, with no entries yet.

The people in a Pot are just names. They are not linked to an account, so you can add someone who does not use veyq.

## The Pot's page

- **Entries**: everything that was paid, paid back or handed over. See [Record an expense](/veyq/expenses/record-an-expense/).
- **Balances**: who is owed and who owes, and a suggestion how to settle up. See [Settle up](/veyq/expenses/settle-up/).
- **Overview**: the totals per category, and per person what they paid beside what their share was.
- **People**: the names in the Pot.
- **Settings** (members of the space only): the base currency, which can be changed only while the Pot has no entry,
  and the Pot's own categories beside the given ones. You can add and rename your own categories, not remove them.

## People

On **People**, whoever may change entries can add a person and rename one. A member of the space can also:

- **Deactivate** a person whose balance is settled. They are no longer offered for new entries, and old entries still
  show their name. You can activate them again.
- **Delete** a person whose balance is settled. Their name is erased, and entries show **deleted people** in its place.
  This cannot be undone.

## If changes are missing

Every entry remembers which entries it was written on top of. If the server leaves out a change that a later entry
refers to, the Pot says above its balances that they may be wrong, and names the entries the missing changes belong to.

## Like every other thing in your space

A Pot can be moved, copied, deleted to the [trash](/veyq/spaces/trash/) and restored like a folder or a file.
[Download](/veyq/spaces/download-and-import/) gives you a file that veyq reads back when you import it into another
folder, and a CSV file of the entries for a spreadsheet. Anyone who can see an entry can
[report it](/veyq/help/report-something/).

If Expenses is [switched off](/veyq/applications/), a banner says so and nothing in a Pot can be added or changed.

## Is it private?

A Pot is locked on your device like everything else in veyq. The server sees that a Pot exists, how many entries it
has, and when they were written and by whom. It does not see an amount, a name in the Pot, a category, a currency, or
who owes whom. Details: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
