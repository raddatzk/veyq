**Expenses** keeps **Pots** for shared costs: a trip, a shared flat, a club evening. Everyone puts in what they paid,
and the Pot shows at any time who paid what and who owes whom.

<Screenshot id="expenses-pot-entries" shows="An opened Pot on its Entries tab, with the tabs Entries, Overview and Settings, and a few entries, each with its title, date, what it is and who paid, and its amount" />

## What you can do

- [Record an expense](/veyq/expenses/record-an-expense/)
- [Settle up](/veyq/expenses/settle-up/)
- [Share a Pot](/veyq/expenses/share-a-pot/)

## Create a Pot

1. Open the folder where the Pot should be.
2. Choose **New**, then **New Pot**.
3. Type a name, choose the **base currency** (what every share is counted in), and add the people in it: type a
   name, or choose a member of the space.
4. Create it. The Pot opens, with no entries yet.

A person in a Pot is either a member of the space or just a name. A name is linked to no account, so you can add
someone who does not use veyq. You can add more people of either kind on **Settings** later.

## The Pot's page

- **Entries**: everything that was paid, paid back or handed over. See [Record an expense](/veyq/expenses/record-an-expense/).
- **Overview**: who is owed and who owes, each person's row saying what they paid, what they consumed and what they
  transferred; a suggestion how to settle up; and the totals per category. See [Settle up](/veyq/expenses/settle-up/).
- **Settings**: the people in the Pot, each with their balance in short. For members of the space also the base
  currency, which can be changed only while the Pot has no entry, and the Pot's own categories beside the given ones.
  You can add and rename your own categories, not remove them.

At the top of the page, whoever may change the space can give the Pot a **cover**: click the empty frame beside its
name, as for a folder ([Give a folder a cover](/veyq/spaces/give-a-folder-a-cover/)). A download or a copy of the Pot
leaves the cover out.

## People

On **Settings**, whoever may change entries can add a person by typing a name, and rename a name. A member of the
space can also add another member of the space: the Pot then shows that person with their current name and profile
picture, and as a **former member** once they have left the space or deleted their account; their entries stay. A
person added as a name is never linked to an account later.

Someone who opens the Pot through a link adds names only. They see a member by the name the member had when they were
added, without a picture.

And a member of the space can:

- **Deactivate** a person whose balance is settled. They are no longer offered for new entries, and old entries still
  show their name. You can activate them again.
- **Delete** a person whose balance is settled. Their name is erased, and entries show **deleted people** in its place.
  This cannot be undone.

## If changes are missing

Every entry remembers which entries it was written on top of. If the server leaves out a change that a later entry
refers to, the Pot says above its balances that they may be wrong, and names the entries the missing changes belong to.

## Like every other thing in your space

A Pot can be moved, copied, deleted to the [trash](/veyq/spaces/trash/) and restored like a folder or a file.
[Download](/veyq/spaces/download-and-import/) gives you a file that veyq reads back when you import it into another
folder, a CSV file of the entries for a spreadsheet, and each [receipt](/veyq/expenses/record-an-expense/#receipts) as a
file of its own. An import and a copy bring the receipts along with their entries. Anyone who can see an entry can
[report it](/veyq/help/report-something/).

If Expenses is [switched off](/veyq/applications/), a banner says so and nothing in a Pot can be added or changed.

## Is it private?

A Pot is locked on your device like everything else in veyq. The server sees that a Pot exists, how many entries it
has, when they were written and by whom, and how many receipts an entry keeps and how large each is. It does not see an
amount, a name in the Pot, a category, a currency, what a receipt shows, or who owes whom. Details: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
