1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then **Storage** under **Shared space**.
2. **In use** shows how much your space takes on the server, the trash included. If the person who runs the server
   set a limit for your space, it shows under **Storage limit of this space**; only they can change it.
3. Under **Limit per upload link (GiB)**, set how much a single link may add: every link through which guests can add
   photos or files gets this limit, counted per link. **0** switches uploads through links off.
4. Choose **Save storage settings**.

<Screenshot id="owners-storage" shows="The Storage panel with In use and the Limit per upload link field" />

The limit keeps a link that travels further than you meant from filling the server. It applies from a link's next
upload; nothing already stored is removed.

Until you change it, each link may add 10 GiB, enough for a party or a wedding. When a link is full, whoever uses it
is told it isn't taking more uploads right now, and nothing of the refused upload is kept. Photos you delete from what
guests added free their room on the link only once they leave the trash.

The server counts sizes, because it has to store the bytes; it never sees what they are:
