As the owner of a shared space you can set terms that every member, and everyone who opens a link to something in
the space, agrees to before they go on.

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then **Terms** under **Shared space**.
2. Choose **Add language**, pick the language and write its terms; repeat for each language you want. Only languages
   not added yet are offered.
3. Change a language's terms with **Edit**, or start from **Insert template**: a draft to adapt before you save. Markdown works
   for headings and lists. The template contains the placeholder `{contact}`; replace it with how people reach you.
   veyq asks before the template fills a language, and warns you if it would replace terms you have already written
   there. Nothing is in force until you save.
4. Check the **Preview** and choose **Save terms**. The page says which revision is now in force.

<Screenshot id="owners-terms" shows="The Terms panel with the Deutsch language, the Insert template and Edit buttons, the Save terms button and the preview" />

Every member, you included, now sees **Please review the terms** the next time they open the space and continues with
**I agree**. Guests opening a link see **Please accept the terms of use** first. Each time you save a change, everyone
is asked again.

When you agree, the server keeps only a small note: which version of the terms you accepted, and when. It never learns
anything about your photos. When someone agrees through a link, that stays in their own browser only.

Members who are not owners can read the terms here but not change them.

## The section of the person who runs the server

After your terms, veyq adds a section of its own: the same on every space on this server, filled in with what the
person who runs the server set. It says how to report something, who looks at a report, what can be done about it,
what is kept and for how long, and how to object, and it points to the privacy notice for what is recorded when a guest
adds something and what rights a guest has; where that person stated nothing, it says *not stated by this operator*. You cannot change or remove it, and the Terms panel shows it below your
own. The starter template leaves these points to it: its clause 9 points there.

Members and guests read this section after your terms and agree to both at once. They are asked again when a veyq
update changes the section, without your revision changing; a value the person who runs the server changes is not a
reason to ask again. If your space has no terms, nobody is asked; the section can still be read under **Terms of this
server** at the bottom of the sign-in page, of every link and of the app.

The terms are shown to people before they see anything else, but they are not a lock on your data; that is the
encryption: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
