Your space sends two kinds of mail: invitations, and the links that let members reset a forgotten password or confirm
their address. Until you set up email, neither can go out. Email is set up per space, by its owner, not by whoever
runs the server.

1. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then **Email** under **Shared space**.
2. Enter your mail server's **SMTP host** and **Port**, the **From address**, and the **Username** and **Password** for
   it. Leave **Use STARTTLS** on unless your mail server says otherwise.
   If your mail server is a relay that accepts mail without a login, leave **Username** and **Password** empty.
3. Turn on **Send invitations by email**.
4. If you like, write your own **Subject** and **Body** for the invitation under **Invitation email**. You can use
   `{{link}}`, `{{domain}}` and `{{expiry}}`; leave both empty for the standard text.
   Whatever you write there goes to everyone you invite exactly as written, and it is stored on the server unlocked,
   so keep it about the invitation.
5. Choose **Save email settings**.

<Screenshot id="owners-email" shows="The Email panel with the line Ready to send email, the SMTP fields, Send invitations by email and the invitation template" />

The top of the panel then says **Ready to send email**; otherwise it lists what is still missing. To check that mail
really arrives, send yourself a confirmation from the offer that appears after saving.

The mail password is stored locked on the server and never shown again; leave the field empty to keep it. The mails
themselves carry only links, never anything you keep in your space:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
