If you own a shared space, you decide who is in it. Everything here is under **Settings → Members**: the menu at the
bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Members** under **Shared space**. Members see the list too; only you
see the buttons.

## Invite someone

Your space must be able to send email first ([Email](/veyq/space-owners/email/)).

1. Choose **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **Email address** and choose **Send invite**.

<Screenshot id="owners-invite" shows="The Invite member dialog with the Email address field and the Send invite button" />

The invitation goes to them by email, and only there. There is no link on your screen to copy or forward: an
invitation is an admission ticket, and a ticket that travels through whoever looks at your screen can end up with the
wrong person. They create their account from the mail:
[Accept an invitation](/veyq/getting-started/accept-an-invitation/).

Under **Pending invites** you see who has not joined yet. **Send again** mails the invitation once more; **Cancel
invite** makes its link stop working.

## After they join

A new member shows **Awaiting access** until the key to the space reaches them. That happens by itself: any member's
device, yours or another one's, locks a copy of the key to the newcomer the next time it is online. Then they show
**Active**. The server only passes the locked copy along; it can never let anyone in behind your back.

## Remove a member

1. Choose **Remove member** on their row.
2. If they might have copied links to things in the space, tick **Also turn off every link in this space**.
3. Confirm.

They lose access to the space and its groups. What they already saw, they may have kept. A group they owned alone
passes on or is closed.

In more detail: a group they ran alone passes to the member who has been in it longest, and a group where they were
the only person is deleted after 30 days. You never gain access to anyone's group this way. Their Personal space is
not taken away at once: for 30 days they can still sign in, look through it and save what they want, but change
nothing. Then it is erased.

Each group they shared with others also gets a new lock. One of its owners' devices fits it by itself the next time
that owner opens veyq. The server shuts them out at once, but until the new lock is in, their old key would still open
what is new in that group. The new lock is on the group itself: folders and Memories that were already there keep
their keys, so their old key would still open what is added inside them.

## Share the ownership

Choose **Make co-owner** on a member's row: they own the shared space with you from then on, and can invite and remove
members too. **Remove co-owner** makes an owner a member again; the last owner cannot step back. Nothing about the
space's lock changes: every member already holds its key.

A second owner is also what keeps the space going if you leave: when you
[erase your account](/veyq/account/erase-your-account/) as its last owner, you choose to make one of its members the
owner or to delete the space along with your account. A space whose last owner's account was erased by whoever runs
your server has no owner until they invite one of its members to own it.

## Give the space an icon

Choose **Add icon** next to the space's name, pick an image, move it into the square and choose **Save icon**. Every
member sees it in the sidebar. **Remove icon** brings back the initial.

The key to your space never reaches the server: members' devices hand it to each other, locked to each one:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
