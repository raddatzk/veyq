If you own a shared space, you decide who is in it. Everything here is under **Settings → Members**: the menu at the
bottom of the sidebar, then **Settings**, then **Members** under **Shared space**. Members see the list too; only you
see the buttons.

## Invite someone

Your space must be able to send email first ([Email](/veyq/space-owners/email/)).

1. Choose **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **Email address** and choose **Send invite**.
3. The dialog shows a **Code for the invitation**, only this once. Choose **Copy code** and send it to them yourself,
   by message or in person, not by email.

<Screenshot id="owners-invite" shows="The Invite member dialog with the Email address field and the Send invite button" />

The invitation link goes to them by email, and only there. The link alone does not open it: the server mails it, so
the code has to travel another way. If you would rather agree on a word between you, choose **Choose a password
instead** before sending, and tell them the password yourself (at least 10 characters). They create their account
from the mail and the code: [Accept an invitation](/veyq/getting-started/accept-an-invitation/).

Under **Pending invites** you see who has not joined yet. **Send again** cancels the old invitation and mails a new
link, with a new code to pass on; the old link and code stop working. **Cancel invite** makes its link stop working.

## After they join

A new member can open the space at once: the invitation carried the key to the space, locked under the code, and
their device took it over when they joined. Nobody has to be online for that, and they show **Active** straight away.

## Remove a member

1. Choose **Remove member** on their row.
2. If they might have copied links to things in the space, tick **Also turn off every link in this space**.
3. If they may have kept a copy of the space's key, for example on a device you can't get back, tick **Also give this
   space a new lock** ([below](#give-the-space-a-new-lock)).
4. Confirm.

They lose access to the space and its groups. What they already saw, they may have kept. A group they owned alone
passes on or is closed.

In more detail: a group they ran alone passes to the member who has been in it longest, and a group where they were
the only person is deleted after 30 days. Only people who accepted count: someone still asked to join is never handed
the group, and their open invitation to it lapses. You never gain access to anyone's group this way. Their Personal space is
not taken away at once: for 30 days they can still sign in, look through it and save what they want, but change
nothing. Then it is erased.

Each group they shared with others also gets a new lock, on every folder and Memory in it. One of its owners'
devices starts fitting it by itself the next time that owner opens veyq, and every member's device helps put it in.
Adding something there takes a moment longer while the lock goes onto that spot; an upload through a link waits until
it is in. The server shuts them out at once, but until the new lock is in, their old key would still open what is new
in that group.
Links made before stay open to them until you renew them or switch them off
([Renew a link](/veyq/sharing/manage-links/)).

## Give the space a new lock

The shared space itself gets no new lock when you remove someone: the server shuts them out at once, and that is enough
as long as you can trust the server. If a member's device was lost, or someone who left may have kept a copy of the
key, give the space a new lock: choose **Give it a new lock** under **New lock** and confirm, or tick it when you remove
the member.

Your device makes the new key and hands it to every member and every open invitation. A member whose key it can't
confirm gets it a little later, from the next member whose device can; until then the space shows **Waiting for the
current key** for them and its contents stay locked. Then the lock goes onto every folder and Memory;
adding something takes a moment longer until it is in. Whoever holds only the old key, a removed member or a lost
device, can't read anything here any more, neither what is added nor what is already here. What they already saw stays seen, and links made before keep working
until you renew them or turn them off. A group's owners give a group a new lock the same way:
[People in a group](/veyq/spaces/group-members/#give-the-group-a-new-lock).

## Share the ownership

Choose **Make co-owner** on a member's row: they own the shared space with you from then on, and can invite and remove
members too. **Remove co-owner** makes an owner a member again; the last owner cannot step back. Nothing about the
space's lock changes: every member already holds its key.

A second owner is also what keeps the space going if you leave: when you
[erase your account](/veyq/account/erase-your-account/) as its last owner, you choose to make one of its members the
owner or to delete the space along with your account. A space whose last owner's account was erased by whoever runs
your server has no owner until they invite one of its members to own it.

## Give the space an icon

Choose **Add icon** next to the space's name, pick an image, move it into the square and choose **Save icon**. Every
member sees it in the sidebar. **Remove icon** brings back the initial.

The key to your space never reaches the server: members' devices hand it to each other, locked to each one:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
