Normally an owner of a group simply adds you, and the group appears in your sidebar. If you would rather be asked
first, turn on **Ask before adding me to groups** in [Your profile](/veyq/account/your-profile/). Being added then
becomes an invitation, and the group does not open for you until you say yes.

1. When you have an invitation, a button with a number appears at the top of the page (**Group invitations**).
   Choose it.
2. Each invitation says which group and who invited you.
3. Choose **Accept** to join, or **Decline**.

<Screenshot id="spaces-group-invitations" shows="The Group invitations page with one invitation, Invited by @sam, and the Accept and Decline buttons" />

After **Accept**, the group appears under **Groups** in the sidebar right away, and you can open it.

The name you see is the group's real name, the same one its members see: whoever invites you cannot show you a
different one, and the server cannot read it. **Decline** removes the invitation, and your answer is not recorded
anywhere. The owner's device locked the group's key to you when it invited you, so a declined invitation gives the
group a new key, as a removal does.

Until you accept, veyq does not hand you that key: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
