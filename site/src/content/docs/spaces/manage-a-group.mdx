Only owners of a group can do this. Open the group under **Settings**: the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then
**Settings**, then the group's name under **Groups**.

## Rename it

Choose **Rename** next to the group's name, enter the new **Group name** and choose **Save**.
The new name is locked on your device like the old one, and someone you removed from the group before the rename
cannot read it.

## Give it an icon

1. Choose **Add icon** (or **Change icon**).
2. Choose **Choose an image**, pick a picture and move it into the square.
3. Choose **Save icon**.

The icon shows in the sidebar instead of the group symbol. **Remove icon** takes it away again. The icon of the shared
space itself is set the same way by its owner, under **Settings → Members**.
Every icon is stored as a locked block of exactly the same size, so the server cannot even tell a plain logo from a
photo.

## Delete it, and change your mind

1. Under **Delete group**, choose **Delete group**.
2. Confirm **Delete this group?** with **Delete**.

The group is not gone at once. For 30 days everyone can still look through it, but nothing new can be added; a notice
in the group says on which day it will be deleted. Until then, any owner can choose **Restore group** in the same
place, and everything is back as it was. After that day it is erased for good.

For those 30 days the group's public links stop working. Whoever opens one sees what a switched-off link shows and
learns nothing about the deletion. If the group is restored, the links work again unchanged.

A group's name and icon are locked with the group's key on your device, so only its members see them:
