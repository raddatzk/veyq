Only owners of a group manage who is in it. Open it under **Settings**: the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then
**Settings**, then the group's name under **Groups**. Only the groups you own are listed there.

## Add someone

Anyone you add must already be a member of the shared space.

1. Under **Members**, type their **username** and choose **Add**.
2. They appear in the list as **Active** and have access right away: your device locks the group's key to them as
   you add them.

<Screenshot id="spaces-group-members" shows="A group's settings with the name and icon at the top, the username field with the Add button, and a member list" />

If they chose **Ask before adding me to groups**, they get an invitation instead, and the list says **Awaiting
consent** until they accept: [Accept a group invitation](/veyq/spaces/group-invitations/). **Withdraw** takes the
invitation back. Because the key was already locked to them, a withdrawn or declined invitation gives the group a new
key, as a removal does.

If veyq says **This person's key doesn't follow from the one you knew**, the person is not added, and nothing is
shared with that key. Compare your safety numbers in person: [A key can't be confirmed](/veyq/help/key-changed/).

## Make someone a co-owner

Choose **Make co-owner** on their row. Co-owners can do everything you can, including adding and removing people.
**Remove co-owner** takes it back; a group always keeps at least one owner.

## Remove someone

1. Choose **Remove** on their row.
2. veyq asks **Remove and rotate the key?** If they might have copied links to things in the group, tick **Also turn
   off every link on this group**.
3. Choose **Remove**.

The group then gets a new key that the removed person does not have, on every folder and Memory in it, so nothing
added from now on reaches them, except through a link made before until you renew it
([Renew a link](/veyq/sharing/manage-links/)). Adding things waits while the new key goes in. What they already saw,
they may have kept.

You cannot leave a group yourself; another owner removes you. You cannot remove yourself either.

A group's key travels from device to device, locked to each member; the server passes it on but cannot open it:
[Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
