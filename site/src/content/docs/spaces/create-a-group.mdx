A group is a smaller circle inside your shared space, for example the grandparents, or the people who went on one
trip. It has a space of its own that only its members can open; the rest of the shared space cannot see into it.

1. In the sidebar, choose the **+** next to **Groups** (**New group**).
2. Enter a **Group name** and choose **Create**.
3. The new group opens. You are its owner and so far its only member.

<Screenshot id="spaces-new-group" shows="The New group dialog with the Group name field and the Cancel and Create buttons" />

Next, [add people to it](/spaces/group-members/). You can also create a group under **Settings → Groups**, with
the **+** there.

If you see no **+**, groups are switched off on your server or in your space.

A group's name and everything in it are locked with the group's own key, which only its members' devices hold:
[Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
