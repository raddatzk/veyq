Whatever you delete in a space, a folder, a Memory, a file or a drop box, goes to that space's trash first, with
everything inside it. It stays there until the date shown, normally 30 days, and is then removed for good, unless
you remove it sooner yourself.

## Bring something back

1. In the space, choose **Trash**: the bin at the right end of the row above the list.
2. Each entry says when it was deleted and when it will be removed for good.
3. Choose **Restore**. It comes back complete, where it was. If its name is taken there by now, enter a **New name**
   first.
4. Choose **Done**.

<Screenshot id="spaces-trash" shows="The Trash window with two deleted entries, each with its deletion date, the date it is removed for good, a Restore and a Delete forever button" />

Right after deleting, the message at the bottom also offers **Undo** for a few seconds.

## Remove something for good now

1. In the trash, choose **Delete forever** beside the entry.
2. veyq asks first, naming it, because this cannot be undone. Confirm with **Delete forever**.

It is gone at once, with everything inside it, and cannot be brought back. Anyone who belongs to the space can do
this, as anyone can delete and restore. Only what is already in the trash can be removed this way; there is no button
that empties the whole trash at once. Until something is gone for good, it still counts towards your space's storage.

Whatever you delete also leaves every link you shared at once: people who open the link no longer see it, or anything
in it. If you restore it, it is back in those links; once it is removed for good, a link to it stops working for good.

Photos you remove inside a Memory have a trash of their own in that Memory:
[Remove photos and bring them back](/veyq/memories/photo-trash/).

What lies in the trash is just as locked as everything else; the server only knows when it may remove it:
