## One list for everything in a folder

A folder shows **one list** of everything in it: sub-folders, Memories, files and drop boxes together. Folders come
first. Everything else follows in alphabetical order, whatever it is; numbers count as numbers, so "Photo 2" comes before
"Photo 10".

Each entry says what it is: for example "File · 157 B · modified …" or "Memory · June 2024". The most useful button (for a
file, download) sits on the entry; the rest are in its **⋯** menu. Where you may not do something, its button is not
there.

## Tabs

When a folder holds more than one kind of thing, tabs above the list — **All**, **Memories**, **Depot**, **Drop boxes** —
show just one kind. Sub-folders stay visible in every tab.

## Grid or list

Use **Grid view** and **List view** at the top of the folder, and **Map view** wherever the tab shows Memories. This
browser remembers your choice.

## Favorites

Every entry has a **heart** (**Favorite**). It adds the entry to **Favorites** in the sidebar, which lists the
favorites of the space you are in and appears once you have one; **Remove favorite** takes it out again. If a favorite
cannot be saved, the heart goes back and a message says so. Your favorites are yours alone: other members of the space cannot see them, and
neither can the server. People you share a link with get no heart.

## Addresses you can bookmark

The address of a folder in your browser reads like its path, for example `…/#holidays/2026/`. A folder's address ends
with a slash. A Memory sits under `/memories/`, for example `…/#holidays/memories/rome`. You can bookmark any of them.
The readable part comes after the `#`, which a browser never sends to a server, so the names in the address stay on your
device.
