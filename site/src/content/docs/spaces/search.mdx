The search field at the top of the page searches every space you belong to here: your personal space, the shared
space and every group. On a phone, choose the magnifier at the top to open it.

1. Click into **Search all spaces**, or press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on
   a Mac).
2. Type part of a name: a folder, a Memory, a chapter, a file.
3. Choose a result. It opens where it lives, and if it is in another space, veyq switches to that space.

## Narrow it down

While you type, veyq offers what your words could mean. Choose one to turn it into a filter; choose its × or press
<kbd>Backspace</kbd> to take it away again.

- **Year**: four digits, such as `2024`. A Memory matches when its dates touch that year.
- **Person**: part of a member's name. It finds the Memories that list them as there.
- **Place**: part of a place a Memory names.
- **Space**: part of a space's name.
- **Kind**: a word such as *Memory*, *Chapter*, *Folder*, *Photo* or *Video*.

## How the results are grouped

- **In “…”**: when a Memory is open, what is in it comes first: its chapters, and its photos and videos.
- **In this space**: the rest of the space you are in.
- **Other spaces**: everything else, each result with its space and where it lives there.

## What is searched

Names, a Memory's dates, its place and the people in it, in all your spaces. Photos and videos are searched in the open
Memory only, by their file name, the day they were taken and their place. A photo in another Memory is not found by its
own date or place yet.

While veyq is still reading a space, the field says so; a space it could not read is named as not searched.

A shared link has no search.
