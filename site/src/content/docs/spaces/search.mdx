The search field at the top of the page searches every space you belong to here: your personal space, the shared
space and every group. On a phone, choose the magnifier at the top to open it.

1. Click into **Search all spaces**, or press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on
   a Mac).
2. Type part of a name: a folder, a Memory, a chapter, a file, a Pot, a bookmark, a person or a part of the settings.
   The list opens with the first letter you type. A single letter finds names and places with a word that starts with
   it; from the second letter on, a name is also found by letters inside a word. The part of each name your words
   matched is shown in bold. If nothing is found, the field says so and names what it searches.
3. Choose a result, or move onto it with <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. It opens where it
   lives, and if it is in another space, veyq switches to that space. A photo or a video opens its Memory with it shown
   large. A person opens their profile; a settings result opens that part of the settings.

## Narrow it down

While you type, veyq offers what your words could mean, under **Filter by**, above the results. Choose one to turn it
into a filter: click it, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> while it is highlighted. The first entry of the list is highlighted,
a filter or a result, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> takes the highlighted entry. Once you move the highlight with <kbd>↑</kbd>,
<kbd>↓</kbd> or the pointer, it stays on that entry while you type on. If your words no longer find that entry, the
first entry of its part of the list - the filters or the results - is highlighted instead; once the entry is back, for example after <kbd>Backspace</kbd>, it is
highlighted again. Choose a filter's × or press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> to take it away again.

- **Year**: four digits, such as `2024`. A Memory matches when its dates touch that year, a photo when it was taken
  in it.
- **Month**: a month's name with its year, such as `May 2024` (or in German, `Mai 2024`); the first three letters of
  the month are enough. A Memory matches when its dates touch that month, a photo when it was taken in it. A month's
  name without a year is read as part of a name, not as a filter.
- **Person**: part of a member's name. It finds the Memories that list them as there, not their profile. The same word
  also lists the member among the results: choose the filter to find their Memories, or the person to open their profile.
- **Place**: part of a place a Memory or a photo names.
- **Space**: part of a space's name.
- **In**: part of the name of a folder, a Memory or a chapter that holds something. It finds what lies inside it, its
  photos included, and only there. Two with the same name are offered as two filters, each with its space and where it
  lies, so you can choose the one you mean.
- **Kind**: a word such as *Memory*, *Chapter*, *Folder*, *File*, *Drop box*, *Pot*, *Bookmark*, *Photo*, *Video*,
  *Person* or *Setting*. *Person* lists only members, *Setting* only parts of the settings.

A filter narrows everything: a member or a part of the settings stays in the list only where it fits the filter, so a
year filter lists no person, as a person has no year.

## How the results are ordered

The results are one list under the filters. What your words match best comes first: a name that is exactly your words,
then one that starts with them, then one with a word that starts with them, then one that holds them inside a word, then
a place or a word of the settings. Among equally good matches, what is near you comes first: what is inside the open
Memory (its chapters, its photos and videos), then the rest of the space you are in, then your other spaces, then
members and the settings. So a name that is exactly your words still comes first when it lies in another space.

Each result says where it lies: its space and its place there, also for the space you are in. A photo shows its Memory and the day it was taken. A member shows their `~username`, and is found by it too;
a part of the settings shows the heading it has there. Besides its name, each part of the settings is found by a few
words of its own: *email* or *SMTP* finds the mail settings, *password* the place to change it. Such a word counts from
the third letter you type, and a part of the settings found by it, not by its name, shows the word after its heading,
the part you typed in bold - so every result shows why it is there. You find exactly the
parts your settings offer you right now: a part only the space's owner can open is listed for its owner alone.

The first 10 results are shown; **Show 10 more** adds the next ones.

## What is searched

Names, a Memory's dates, its place and the people in it, and every photo and video by its file name, the day it was
taken and its place, in all your spaces, and the members and the settings. A photo the operator has blocked is not
found.

If veyq is still reading a space after a moment, the field says so; a space it could not read is named as not searched.

With a screen reader, the field is a combobox: the number of results is read out as it changes, and so is a filter
added or taken away; the filters are named by their heading, and the results by their own name.

A shared link has no search.
