The search field at the top of the page searches every space you belong to here: your personal space, the shared
space and every group. On a phone, choose the magnifier at the top to open it.

1. Click into **Search all spaces**, or press <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on
   a Mac).
2. Type part of a name: a folder, a Memory, a chapter, a file, a Pot, a bookmark, a person or a part of the settings.
3. Choose a result, or move onto it with <kbd>↑</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd> and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. It opens where it
   lives, and if it is in another space, veyq switches to that space. A photo or a video opens its Memory with it shown
   large. A person opens their profile; a settings result opens that part of the settings.

## Narrow it down

While you type, veyq offers what your words could mean, under **Filter by**, above the results. Choose one to turn it
into a filter: click it, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> while it is highlighted. The first entry of the list is highlighted,
a filter or a result, and <kbd>Enter</kbd> takes the highlighted entry. Once you move the highlight with <kbd>↑</kbd>,
<kbd>↓</kbd> or the pointer, it stays on that entry while you type on. If your words no longer find that entry, the
first entry of its group is highlighted instead; once the entry is back, for example after <kbd>Backspace</kbd>, it is
highlighted again. Choose a filter's × or press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> to take it away again.

- **Year**: four digits, such as `2024`. A Memory matches when its dates touch that year, a photo when it was taken
  in it.
- **Person**: part of a member's name. It finds the Memories that list them as there. The same word also lists the
  member under **People**: choose the filter to find their Memories, or the person to open their profile.
- **Place**: part of a place a Memory or a photo names.
- **Space**: part of a space's name.
- **Kind**: a word such as *Memory*, *Chapter*, *Folder*, *File*, *Drop box*, *Pot*, *Bookmark*, *Photo* or *Video*.

## How the results are grouped

- **In “…”**: when a Memory is open, what is in it comes first: its chapters, and its photos and videos.
- **In this space**: the rest of the space you are in.
- **Other spaces**: everything else, each result with its space and where it lives there. A photo stands where its
  Memory stands, with its Memory and the day it was taken.
- **People**: everyone who is a member here, found by their name or by their `~username`.
- **Settings**: the parts of the settings, each with the heading it has there. You find exactly the ones your
  settings offer you right now: a part only the space's owner can open is listed for its owner alone.

While a filter is set, People and Settings are not listed: a filter narrows what is in your spaces.

## What is searched

Names, a Memory's dates, its place and the people in it, and every photo and video by its file name, the day it was
taken and its place, in all your spaces, and the members and the settings. A photo the operator has blocked is not
found.

While veyq is still reading a space, the field says so; a space it could not read is named as not searched.

A shared link has no search.
