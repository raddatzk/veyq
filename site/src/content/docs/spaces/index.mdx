Everything you keep lives in a **space**. The sidebar lists yours under **Spaces**:

- **The shared space**, with its name and address, for example "Example Family". Every member of it sees what is in
  it, and every member may add to it, rename, move and delete.
- **Personal**, your own space. Only you can open what is in it.
- **Groups**, listed below under **Groups**: smaller circles of members. Each has a space only its members can open.

Click one to open it. Inside, a space is organised in folders, like the files on your computer.

veyq opens in the shared space, or in Personal until the key to the shared space has reached you. Personal has its own
address you can bookmark: after the `#` comes `~` and your username, for example `…/#~sam`; `…/#~me` works too.

<Screenshot id="spaces-sidebar" shows="The sidebar with the shared space and Personal under Spaces, two groups under Groups, and Favorites" />

If you belong to more than one shared space, for example your family's and your club's, choose **Switch space** next
to the shared space's name and pick another. Each shared space has its own address, its own Personal space and its
own groups.

Each space has its own key, and only its members' devices hold it. That is why a group is closed even to the other
members of the shared space, and your Personal space even to the person who runs the server:
[Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)

To someone who is not in a space, veyq answers as if the space did not exist. It does not even tell them whether the
space is being deleted or has been taken offline.
