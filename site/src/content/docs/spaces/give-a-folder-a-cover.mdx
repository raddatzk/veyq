A folder, a drop box, a Memory or a Pot can have a **cover**: a picture of your choice that replaces its icon in the
list, on its page and in your favorites. A file has no cover.

1. Open the **⋯** menu of the entry and choose **Set cover** - or, on its page, click the picture at the top left: without
   a cover it is an empty frame that says **Set cover**. It says **Change cover** once there is one.
2. Choose **Choose an image** and pick a picture from your device.
3. Drag and zoom it into the frame you like.
4. Choose **Save cover**.

<Screenshot id="depot-cover-dialog" shows="The Cover dialog on a folder with a picture in the frame, Choose an image, Remove cover and Save cover" />

The window is the same for a folder, a drop box, a Memory and a Pot. Open **Change cover** again to frame it differently or swap
the picture. **Remove cover** in that window takes it away, and the old picture is deleted from the server's storage
(unless it is a photo the Memory's gallery still holds, see [Choose a cover](/memories/set-a-cover/)).
Copies that other people's browsers already hold, or a link that was already handed out, cannot be taken back; they
expire on their own.
Swapping in a different picture deletes the old one from the server's storage in the same way; framing it differently
keeps the picture you have.

Only people who may change the space can set a cover. People you share a link with see the covers but cannot set them.
The picture is shrunk on your device and locked before it leaves it, like everything else.
