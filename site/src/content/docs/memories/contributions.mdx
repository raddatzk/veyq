In a Memory that several people add to, the **Contributions** tab groups the photos and videos by the person who added
them: you, another member, or someone who added through a public link.

1. Open the Memory and choose the **Contributions** tab.
2. Look for a person's group. Each member's group, yours too, shows their name (yours says **You** until you have
   added something). Someone who added through a link
   shows under the name they typed in, or as **Contribution** if they gave none.

<Screenshot id="memories-contributions-tab" shows="The Contributions tab with the groups You, one member and one link contributor" />

It is only another way of looking at the same Memory. Nothing is moved or changed. The names people type in when they
add through a link are shown to the people the Memory belongs to, never to others who open the link.

The grouping happens on your device. The names, including the ones people type in when they add through a link, are
stored locked, so the server never reads them.

## Sort your own photos into sub-buckets

Inside your own group you can keep things tidy:

1. Choose **New sub-bucket**, type a **Name** (for example "Grandma (WhatsApp)") and save.
2. Select photos, or open a photo's menu, and choose **Move item**; then pick the **Target bucket**. **Move item to trash** is in the same menu.
3. **Rename** and **Delete** are in the bucket's menu. Deleting a bucket moves its photos back to your main group;
   nothing is deleted.
