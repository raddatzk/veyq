In a Memory that several people add to, the **Contributions** tab groups the photos and videos by the person who added
them: you, another member, or someone who added through a public link.

1. Open the Memory and choose the **Contributions** tab.
2. Look for a person's group. Each member's group, yours too, shows their name (yours says **You** until you have
   added something). Someone who added through a link
   shows under the name they typed in, or as **Contribution** if they gave none.

<Screenshot id="memories-contributions-tab" shows="The Contributions tab with the groups You, one member and one link contributor" />

It is only another way of looking at the same Memory. Nothing is moved or changed. The names people type in when they
add through a link are shown to the people the Memory belongs to, never to others who open the link.

The grouping happens on your device. The names, including the ones people type in when they add through a link, are
stored locked, so the server never reads them.

## Sort photos into sub-buckets

Any member of the space may tidy any person's group, not only their own:

1. Choose a person's main group, then **New sub-bucket**; type a **Name** (for example "Grandma (WhatsApp)") and save.
2. Select photos, or open a photo's menu, and choose **Move item**; then pick the **Target bucket**. A photo moves only
   between a person's main group and that person's sub-buckets. **Move item to trash** is in the same menu.
3. A sub-bucket has **Rename** and **Delete** beside the group picker; a person's main group has neither. Deleting a
   sub-bucket moves its photos back to that person's main group; nothing is deleted.

## Fix the time of several photos at once

A camera whose clock was wrong, say one still on another time zone, is wrong by the same amount on every photo it
took. You fix one of them, and the others follow:

1. In the **Contributions** tab, select the photos (the box in each photo's corner, or **Select all**).
2. Open one of them whose right time you know, choose **Edit capture time** in its **Details** and set the time it was
   really taken. Or choose **Shift capture time**, the clock above the photos: it shows only the selected photos, with the
   time ready to edit; page to the one whose right time you know.
3. Choose **Save**. Because the photo is one of several selected, veyq asks: **Only this one**, or **All 3, shifted by
   +1 h 12 min** — every selected photo moves by the amount you just moved this one.

- A photo that has no capture time at all stays as it is; veyq says how many there are.
- If someone else changed one of the photos' times in the meantime, that photo keeps their time, and a message names it.
- **Undo** in the message that follows puts every photo's time back as it was.
- A corrected photo shows at the end of the Contributions tab for now; the **Gallery** shows the photos in their new
  order straight away.

The new times are worked out and locked on your device, one photo at a time. The server stores them as it stores any
correction and never learns by how much they moved.

## In the app

In the veyq [app](/getting-started/install-the-app/) **Contributions** is the fourth choice beside **Photos**, **Music**
and **Map**, for the members of the space. Choose a person's group; a photo opened from it pages through that group
only.

- **New sub-bucket** makes a sub-bucket in the group you chose. Any member of the space may tidy any person's group,
  not only their own. A sub-bucket has **Rename** and **Delete**; to delete it, you type its name.
- **To move a photo**, hold it (on a computer, right-click it) and choose **Move item**. A photo moves only between a
  person's main group and that person's sub-buckets.
- **Someone who added through a link** is named in a photo's details by the name they typed, marked as a guest.
- **Offline** the groups and the names stay as the app last saw them, and nothing can be sorted.
