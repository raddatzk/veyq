A Memory, and each of its chapters, can have background music: one **Spotify** playlist, one **Apple Music** playlist, or
one of each. Whoever looks at the Memory can listen to it with their own account while they browse the photos.

## Choose the playlists

1. Open the Memory and choose **Background music** in the **⋯** menu at the top of its page.
2. Paste the share link of a playlist: in Spotify, **Share** → **Copy link to playlist**; in Apple Music, **Share** →
   **Copy Link**. Each service has its own field. A link that is not a playlist of that service (an album, a song,
   another site) is refused, and nothing is saved.
3. Choose **Save**. To remove a playlist, empty its field and save.

Which playlist you chose is locked together with the Memory on your device; veyq's server cannot read it. A chapter has
its own music; it does not play its Memory's.

## Listen

A Memory with a playlist has a button for it at the top of its page, next to **Share**: one for Spotify, one for Apple
Music.

- Press it, and the playlist starts in the **music bar** at the very bottom of the page, below the links. The first time
  in this browser, veyq asks first: the bar says which service it is and that the service then learns your IP address,
  your account with it and this playlist. Nothing of the service is loaded before you choose **Play here**. Your choice
  is remembered in this browser, for that service.
- Sign in with the service in the bar (its own window opens), then press play. Previous, pause and next track are there
  too, and so is what is playing and a link to open it in the service.
- The music keeps playing while you move on: another Memory, another space, your settings. Opening another Memory with
  music changes nothing until you press its button; then its playlist takes over.
- **Close the player** in the bar stops the music. Pressing the Memory's button again does the same. Signing out or locking veyq
  stops it too.

Playing here needs **Spotify Premium** or an **Apple Music subscription**; without one, the player says so and the link
to the service's app stays. Whoever runs your server decides whether music can be played here at all: if they have not
set it up, or switched it off, the Memory's button is **Open in Spotify** or **Open in Apple Music**, which opens the
playlist in the service's own app or website; nothing of the service is loaded into veyq.

## Turn it off

**Turn music off** in the bar ends it at once: the player is removed, the service's sign-in in it is forgotten, and veyq
asks again before the next time. Whoever views the Memory through a link has the same buttons, the same bar at the bottom
of the page and the same choices.

## Who learns what

The service's player runs on a separate address of your server (its music host), apart from veyq itself, so it never
sees your photos, your other Memories or anything you unlocked. veyq's server never sees which playlist you chose, nor
your sign-in with the service. The service sees what any player of its own sees: your IP address, your account, the
playlist and what you listen to.
