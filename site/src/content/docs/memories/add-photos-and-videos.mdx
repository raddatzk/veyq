1. Open the Memory.
2. Choose **Upload** in the buttons at the top (or in the **⋯** menu at their end), and pick your photos and videos. You can also drop them onto the
   Memory.
3. Watch them arrive. Each picture says what it is doing: **Queued**, **Preparing…**, **Uploading…** and then
   **Done**. If one fails it says **Failed**, and you can **Retry** it.

<Screenshot id="memories-upload-progress" shows="The gallery with three tiles that are uploading, showing Queued, Preparing and Uploading" />

Getting a photo ready takes a moment before anything is sent, so a big batch does not start moving the instant you
pick it. That is normal.

The **Uploads** menu in the top bar lists everything that is on its way, one list per Memory. What you add to the
same Memory joins its list until you close it. There you can **Cancel** any upload, or **Cancel all**.

You can keep working while uploads run. In a space you share, the others see your photos appear in the gallery.
Photos are placed by when they were taken, so one that is still uploading already sits in its right place.

Videos work the same way. If your space has no room left, veyq says so; photos in a trash still take up room until
they are removed for good.
