A Memory can show **when** it happened.

1. Open the Memory.
2. Choose the date in the header (it says **No date yet** until there is one), or **Edit date**.
3. Choose how the date should work:
   - **Automatic** takes it from your photos, from the earliest to the latest. It says "Derived from your photos."
     once there are dated photos; until then it asks you to add some or to set a date yourself.
   - **Single day**: pick the **Day**.
   - **Range**: pick **From** and **To**. The end cannot be before the start.
   - **Ongoing**: pick the **Start**, and the Memory shows "Since …".
4. Choose **Save**.

<Screenshot id="memories-date-dialog" shows="The Date dialog with the four choices Automatic, Single day, Range and Ongoing, and the date fields of Range" />

The date is locked together with the Memory on your device. The server never learns the day you were there.
If two people change the date at the same time, veyq shows you what the other person chose and lets you decide again.
