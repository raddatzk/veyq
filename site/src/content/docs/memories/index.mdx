**Memories** is for the photos and videos that belong to an occasion: a trip, a wedding, a birthday, a summer. You
put them together in one **Memory**, give it a date, a place and a cover, and say who was there. A long occasion can
be split into **chapters**, such as "Day 1" and "Day 2". In a space you share with others, everyone who belongs to it
can add to a Memory and look through it, and you can [share](/sharing/) a Memory with people who have no account.

Moving, copying, deleting and sharing a whole Memory work as they do for everything else in your space; they are
described under [Spaces](/spaces/), because they are the same for files, folders and drop boxes.

Several people can work on the same Memory at once. What one of you sets, such as the date, the place, the cover, who
was there or a photo's capture time, is never wiped by someone else's edit, nor by veyq updating the Memory on its
own. If two of you change the very same thing, veyq shows you what the other person chose and lets you decide again.

Your photos, their dates, places and names are locked on your device before they leave it. The server only ever
holds scrambled data: [Is my data safe?](/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
