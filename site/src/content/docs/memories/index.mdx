**Memories** is for the photos and videos that belong to an occasion: a trip, a wedding, a birthday, a summer. You
put them together in one **Memory**, give it a date, a place and a cover, and say who was there. A long occasion can
be split into **chapters**, such as "Day 1" and "Day 2". In a space you share with others, everyone who belongs to it
can add to a Memory and look through it, and you can [share](/veyq/sharing/) a Memory with people who have no account.

## What you can do

- [Create a Memory and chapters](/veyq/memories/create-a-memory-and-chapters/)
- [Add photos and videos](/veyq/memories/add-photos-and-videos/)
- [Look through the gallery](/veyq/memories/the-gallery/)
- [Set the date](/veyq/memories/set-the-date/)
- [Set the location](/veyq/memories/set-the-location/)
- [Say who was there](/veyq/memories/people-in-your-memories/)
- [Choose a cover](/veyq/memories/set-a-cover/)
- [Mark the best photos](/veyq/memories/marked-photos/)
- [See who added what](/veyq/memories/contributions/)
- [Remove photos and bring them back](/veyq/memories/photo-trash/)

Moving, copying, deleting and sharing a whole Memory work as they do for everything else in your space; they are
described under [Spaces](/veyq/spaces/), because they are the same for files, folders and drop boxes.

Your photos, their dates, places and names are locked on your device before they leave it. The server only ever
holds scrambled data: [Is my data safe?](/veyq/privacy/is-my-data-safe/)
