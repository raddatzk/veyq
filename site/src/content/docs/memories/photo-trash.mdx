## Remove a photo

1. Open the photo in the gallery.
2. Choose **Move to trash**. A message offers **Undo** right away.

In the **Contributions** tab you can also select several photos and move them to the trash together.

## Bring it back

1. Open the Memory and choose the **Trash** tab.
2. Select the photo and choose **Restore**.

<Screenshot id="memories-photo-trash" shows="The Trash tab of a Memory with two photos, each showing who removed it, and the Restore and Delete forever buttons" />

Deleted photos stay in the trash for 30 days. After that they are removed for good, automatically. To remove one
earlier, choose **Delete forever**; veyq asks first, because that cannot be undone. Space in the trash still counts
against your storage until the photos are gone for good.

In a Memory that several people share, anyone can tidy up, and the trash shows who removed what, so nothing vanishes
without a trace.
