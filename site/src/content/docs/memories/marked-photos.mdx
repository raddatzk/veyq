Mark the photos you like best with a heart and show only those.

1. Open the Memory.
2. Click the heart on a photo's tile. Its tooltip says **Mark this photo — everyone in this space sees it**. Click
   it again (**Remove your mark**) to take it back.
3. Above the grid, under **Show marked photos**, choose **All**, **Everyone's** or **Only mine**.

<Screenshot id="memories-marked-photos" shows="The gallery with hearts on some tiles, a number beside one heart, and the filter All, Everyone's, Only mine above the grid" />

**A mark is not private.** In a space you share, the others see that a photo was marked, and the heart shows how many
people marked it. veyq does not show who it was, but it cannot hide it either. Treat a mark as something you do in
front of the group. In your personal space there is nobody to share with, so the filter is not shown.

The heart on the **Memory itself** (in the folder list and on its page) is the other way round: that is your favorite,
and nobody else can see it.
