The **Gallery** tab is the first thing you see when you open a Memory: all its photos and videos as a grid.

## Look at a photo

1. Open a Memory.
2. Click a photo. It opens large; use **Previous** and **Next**, or swipe, to go through the rest.
3. A video has a small play badge on its tile. Open it and choose **Play video**.
4. Choose **Details** to see what the picture knows about itself: the file and its size, the camera and lens, how it was
   exposed, who made it, and when it was **Taken**. If it was taken with location switched on, you also see **where**:
   as coordinates, or, once you have [enabled maps](/memories/set-the-location/), as a place name and a small
   map.
5. Double-click a photo, or pinch it, to **zoom** in. Choose **Start slideshow** to move on by itself every five
   seconds; it stops as soon as you touch anything.
6. **Download original** gives you the picture in full quality.

The grid and the large view use compact copies that your device made, so they are quick. The full-quality original is
fetched only when you download it or zoom in further than the compact copy shows. A video plays piece by piece, so only
the parts you watch are fetched.

## With the keyboard

Press **Tab** to move into the grid: it is a single stop, on one photo. The **arrow keys** move from photo to photo,
**Home** and **End** jump to the first and the last, **Page Up** and **Page Down** move by a screen. **Enter** opens the
photo, and when you close it you are back on the photo you looked at last. **Tab** again goes through that photo's own
buttons and then out of the grid. In the **Trash** and **Contributions** tabs, **Space** selects the photo.

However many photos a Memory holds, the grid draws only the ones near what you see, and fetches their small copies
first; the rest appear as you scroll to them.

## Order

Photos are ordered by when they were taken. The button above the grid switches between **Newest first** and **Oldest
first**. If the camera's time is wrong, open the photo's **Details** and choose **Edit capture time**; **Reset to
original** brings the camera's time back.

## Narrow the grid

The field above the grid (**Day, place, name …**) narrows it to what you are looking for. Start typing and choose a
suggestion:

- a **day** the photos were taken on, such as *May 3*;
- a **year**, when the Memory spans more than one;
- a **place** the photos carry;
- **Photo** or **Video**, when the Memory has both.

Each choice becomes a chip in the field, and you can combine several. Whatever you type without choosing a suggestion
matches the photo's file name. The chips also combine with the [marked photos](/memories/marked-photos/) filter. Opened
from the narrowed grid, a photo pages only through what is left. If nothing is left, the gallery says which filters are
in force; remove a chip with its **×** or with Backspace. The filter is for you alone and goes when you leave the
Memory. Someone who opened the Memory through a link has no filter field.

## Live

In a Memory that others can add to, you see their photos appear, and their removals disappear, without reloading.
The same holds for someone who opened it through a link: their gallery stays current as yours does. What the link
tells them changes only when what it shows changes — not when something elsewhere in the space does.
