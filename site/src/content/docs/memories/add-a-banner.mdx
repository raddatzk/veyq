title: "Add a banner"

A banner is a wide picture or a video at the top of a Memory, above its title. It is separate from the cover: the cover is
the Memory's face on cards and on the map, the banner is how the Memory opens. A Memory and each of its chapters can
have one.

1. Open the Memory and choose **Add banner** among the buttons at the top of its page (or in the **⋯** menu at their end). It says
   **Change banner** once there is one.
2. Choose **Choose an image or a video** and pick a file. An image may be up to 40 MB; a video can be as long as you
   like.
3. Two previews show how the banner looks on a **Wide screen** and on a **Phone**. There is nothing to adjust: the
   banner always shows the whole picture or video.
4. Choose **Save banner**. A large file takes a moment to upload; **Cancel** stops it and leaves the old banner as it
   was.

## A photo or video that is already in the gallery

If the file is a photo or video the gallery of the same Memory or chapter already holds, veyq does not store it a
second time: the banner uses that photo's or video's stored copy. It works the other way round too: a video you add to
the gallery that is already the banner is not stored again.

If you delete the photo or video the banner shows, veyq asks first: **Delete the banner's picture?** The banner stays,
unless you also choose **Remove the banner too**.

## Always the whole picture

The banner is wide on a large screen (16:9) and square on a phone, and never cuts anything off your picture or video.
Where it does not fill the banner (at the sides of a tall one, above and below a wide one), a blurred copy of it
fills the rest.

## Videos

A video banner shows its first picture and a play button. Nothing plays until someone presses play. If your browser
cannot show a picture from a video, it says so, and that video cannot be the banner on this device.

## Change or remove it

Open **Change banner** to pick another file. **Remove banner** takes it away.

Only people who may change the space can set a banner. People you share a link with see the banner but cannot change
it. The server stores the banner encrypted: it never sees the picture. Where the banner uses the stored copy
of a photo or video from the gallery, the server can tell which one it is.
