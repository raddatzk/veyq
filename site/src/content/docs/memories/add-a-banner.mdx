A banner is a wide picture or a video at the top of a Memory, above its title. It is separate from the cover: the cover is
the Memory's face on cards and on the map, the banner is how the Memory opens. A Memory and each of its chapters can
have one.

## From a picture or video on your device

1. Open the Memory and choose **Add banner** among the buttons at the top of its page. It says **Change banner** once
   there is one.
2. Choose **Choose an image or a video** and pick a file. An image may be up to 40 MB; a video can be as long as you
   like.
3. Click or drag in the picture to set the **Point that stays in view**. The two previews below show how the banner
   looks on a **Wide screen** and on a **Phone**.
4. If you like, write a **Description**. Screen readers read it in place of the picture.
5. Choose **Save banner**. A large file takes a moment to upload; **Cancel** stops it and leaves the old banner as it
   was.

## From a photo or video of the Memory

1. Open a photo or video in the gallery.
2. Choose **Use as banner** in the toolbar of the large view.
3. Set the point that stays in view and save.

The banner gets its own copy: the photo or video stays in the gallery as it is. The one the banner was made from says
**Current banner**. If you delete it later, veyq tells you that the banner keeps its copy and offers **Remove the
banner too**.

## Tall pictures and videos

A picture or video that is taller than wide is shown whole in the banner, with its sides filled by a blurred copy of
itself. Choose **Fill** instead to let it cover the whole banner around the point you set; **Fit** goes back.

## Videos

A video banner shows its first picture and a play button. Nothing plays until someone presses play. If your browser
cannot show a picture from a video, it says so, and that video cannot be the banner on this device.

## Change or remove it

Open **Change banner** to move the point, switch between fit and fill, change the description or pick another file.
**Remove banner** takes it away.

Only people who may change the space can set a banner. People you share a link with see the banner but cannot change
it. The server stores the banner encrypted: it never learns how you framed it or what you wrote about it.
