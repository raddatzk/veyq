A **drop box** is a place where people can hand you files without seeing anything else and without an account. You send
them a link; they type a name, pick their files, and are done. Each person gets a folder of their own, so nobody sees
what others sent. You and the other members of your space see everything in one place.

## Create a drop box and a link

1. Open the folder where the drop box should be and choose **Share** at the top of its page, then **New share**.
2. Under **What can people do?**, choose **Add files only**.
3. Type a **Name**: it becomes the drop box's name, and people with the link see it. A password and an expiry date are
   optional.
4. Choose **Create link**. This creates the drop box in the folder and its first link in one step.
5. Choose **Copy link**, send the link, and choose **Done**.

To put a drop box at the top of your space, open the space without opening a folder and choose **Share** there; it
offers only **Add files only**. You see this choice only where your space allows drop boxes and you may add things.

The drop box then appears in the folder's list. Its links are kept on the drop box, not in the folder's **Share**
window: open the drop box and choose **Share link** to create another link or manage them in **Drop box links**.

Anyone with the link can add files. Nobody with it sees the drop box or what others added. You can also
[share](/sharing/) a folder that holds a drop box.

## What the sender sees

The page says **Send files**. The sender types a name (the owner sees the files under it), chooses **Continue**, then **Choose files**, and
sees each file go through **Sending…** to **Sent**. If a file does not go through it says why and offers **Try again**. After
leaving, they cannot see their files again. **Not you?** lets them start over under a new name, in a fresh folder.

## What you see

The drop box has one folder per sender, named as they typed it, and the list shows how many people sent something. Open a
person's folder to download or delete their files.

- To remove everything someone sent, delete their folder.
- To keep it as an ordinary folder instead, choose **Move … out…**. After that the sender can no longer add anything
  to it, and it cannot be moved back.

## Switch a link off

In **Drop box links**, **Switch off** stops the link; veyq asks first. What already arrived stays. **Switch on again** brings
it back, unless someone who has left the space may still hold it; then create a new link. **Delete link** is for good.

Deleting the whole drop box sends it to the trash with every sender's folder, and its links take no files while it is there.

The files are locked in the sender's browser; the person running the server cannot read them or their names.
