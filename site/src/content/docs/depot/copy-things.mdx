**Copy to…** sits next to **Move to…** on a folder, a file, a Memory and a drop box, and opens the same window.

1. Open the **⋯** menu of the thing and choose **Copy to…**.
2. Open the place the copy should go to. This can be the place it is in now; the copy is then called "Name (2)".
3. Read what veyq says: how many items and how much data will be copied, what does not come along, and whether your
   space still has room.
4. Choose **Copy here**.

<Screenshot id="depot-copy-dialog" shows="The Copy dialog with the summary of items and size, the list of what does not come along, and the Cancel and Copy here buttons" />

**What does not come along:** shares, favorites, the trash and the original upload times, and a folder's cover. You
become the owner of the copy. A copied drop box becomes an ordinary folder that nobody can drop files into. A copied
Memory keeps its photos and videos with their dates, places and camera details, and its chapters, date, place and
cover. Who was there, and who added each photo, do not come along.

Everything is copied on your device and stored again, so a big folder takes as long as uploading it and uses its space
twice. If the copy stops part-way, what it had made goes to the trash and veyq says how far it got.

Only members of the space can copy. Somebody you shared a folder with cannot.
